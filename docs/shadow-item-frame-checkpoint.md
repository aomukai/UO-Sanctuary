Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**, with approved Sanctuary deviations taking precedence. Coverage remains **208/240 handlers; Assassin 28/30**. This fixes shared equipped-NPC state reconstruction; it adds no learned skill route.

The intermittent original-item event mismatch reported in the [Psychic Hammer checkpoint](shadow-psychic-checkpoint.md) is reproduced and fixed. The existing source weapon-selection routine changes which original hand contributes damage-related stats. The resource ledger previously saved active item identities and event registrations without saving that damage-inclusion state. Rebuilding after a skill-book change replayed both claws with their initial exclusions, losing selected-hand damage and sometimes its event registrations. The strict event-history validator correctly rejected that mismatch.

The ledger now captures a separate, validated subset of active original items whose damage-related stats are included. Reconstruction restores their flags through the existing source merge writer before validating event membership. Skill/buff changes, equipment changes, difficulty previews and saves capture the actual live frame. Ordinary stat reads retain the existing live graph, including during item callbacks. No synthetic items, copied weapon bonuses, relaxed event validator or alternative event ordering are introduced. A duplicate Shadow resistance refresh was also removed.

The actual saved inclusion subset preserves those registrations and numeric fields through Venom expiry and skill-book rebuilding. Both hand selections are checked. The fixture deletes temporary actors and restores the actual world allocation stream afterward.

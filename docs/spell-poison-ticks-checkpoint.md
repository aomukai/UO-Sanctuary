`SpellPoisonTicks` retains the raw poison rate, captured masteries and poison resistance, source, duration and frame phase in the existing `TimedEffects` actor. Positive poison uses the existing rate/length clock. An immune target has a separate zero-rate exposure, without fabricated poison damage, poison status or suppression of normal regeneration. The shared scheduler advances it; there is no unrelated timer. Potion eligibility is checked at each tick. Drinking later, removing the buff or reaching its deadline changes subsequent additions.

The original rate supplies all four additions independently. Original and added poison combine before the poison resistance percentage; the bonus poison causes no new status. Fire, cold and lightning use their own existing defense and absorption paths. Base poison and additions enter one health payment, preserving the existing Protection boundary and the signed NPC poison/item-replenishment aggregation. These ticks bypass ordinary hit recovery and damage callbacks. Existing original poison attribution is retained; cross-owner replacement/credit still needs dedicated acceptance.

The user confirms that cures stop remaining additions and that overlapping spells follow the strongest poison only. Cures and Cleansing now shorten/remove the exposure too, including on immune targets. A weaker successful-rate application retains the winning exposure and reschedules the NPC frame phase alongside the original clock. A stronger/equal application replaces it; a winning nonspell poison removes spell eligibility. Zero-rate applications cannot replace an active positive poison. Deleted or dead sources cannot supply an active potion, while original poison retains its existing lifetime/credit behavior.

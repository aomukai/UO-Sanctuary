# Complete Blood Boil action rule

Prepared Sanctuary adaptation against owned **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. [Source review](../tools/reference/blood-boil-source-review.json). The owned table supplies separate physical/fire endpoints, independent hard Engorge/Blood Oath synergies, demon-count life-cost curves and a three-frame delay. Current do160 remains unreadable. **These missing callback/world rules are explicit proposed adaptations; learned admission stays gated until approved.**

1. **Caster and action.** Require a live connected Warlock/Witch outside actual town/safe areas, an available Blood Boil rank and at least one qualifying demon above the life threshold below. Cast around the demons; no enemy or clicked pet is required. Reuse the shared SC/FCR clock, ordinary resource/form/overlap checks and movement/weapon/incapacitation cancellation. Moving away and back cancels. Require enough mana to start, spend none during windup, recapture the skill's numbers at release and pay mana once for a committed cast. Rank 1 costs 3.75 mana, with +0.25 per additional effective rank. No added local cooldown. Late loss of all qualifying demons or mana costs nothing; spent action time is not refunded. No replay or rollback of already-completed native effects.
2. **Roster and divisor.** Reuse original active learned Goatman/Tainted/Defiler eligibility and their saved creation order. Bodies must be alive, same facet, within 19 native tiles of their owner, unparked, outside town, permitted, fault-free and backed by their original native host; equipment and unqualified legacy contexts remain excluded. Capture the count of this roster, including low-life bodies that cannot erupt. One/two/three active demons select the corresponding owned `calc1/2/3`. Do not invent a future Bind Demon actor or a fourth cost formula. No extra owner-to-pet LOS requirement.
3. **Life cost and threshold.** Only demons with **strictly more than 5% of their current maximum life** erupt; recheck each body before committing its eruption. Its cost is the selected percentage of its current maximum life, truncated in FP8. At rank 1, one demon pays 15%; two each pay 7% after integer division; three each pay 5%. Each curve floors at 5% at higher ranks. Low-life bodies remain in the divisor but pay nothing and do not erupt. If none can erupt, mana is not spent.
4. **A cost can kill.** A demon above the 5% threshold can still have less life than its cost. Consume its remaining life, apply the existing sub-one-HP death floor and use native death cleanup with itself as killer. Its captured center and explosion still resolve. For example, one rank-1 demon at 10% life dies to its 15% maximum-life cost and still explodes. This follows the older observed gate as an explicit rule, not a claim about the current protected callback's rounding edge. The life charge is a resource operation through the existing FP8 setter and death finisher: no resistance, armor, shield, incoming attack event, reflection, leech, damage aggro or invented attack is applied to the pet.
5. **Damage and rolls.** Capture the existing getters' physical/fire endpoints at release. Hard Engorge points add 20% physical damage per point; hard Blood Oath points add 20% fire per point. Soft points do not supply those synergies. Preserve physical pre-shift truncation and elemental FP8 precision. Start one transient cast RNG stream through the existing spell seed supplier; in saved demon order, roll physical then fire once for each erupting demon using the existing source spell roll. Every enemy accepted for that eruption shares its rolled packet. Do not consume the pet's AI/combat RNG or multiply damage by pet life or weapon damage. The original living caster supplies spell credit and the existing spell enhancement/potion/recipient-defense pipeline; no weapon procs or leech are added.
6. **Area.** Center each instantaneous explosion at its demon's captured native point. Use raw `aurarangecalc`: 6 source units before rank 5, 9 at ranks 5–9, 12 from rank 10. Apply the existing source-area circle, height, hostility, town and center-to-target LOS helper, with serial ordering. This is the declared native projection: the owned tooltip separately shows 5/6/7 yards and older notes report an 11-yard cap, so none of those values is silently substituted for the raw damage formula. Empty areas still consume life and mana and show the explosion.
7. **Shared three-frame delay.** For a given caster and enemy, all its demon explosions and Blood Boil casts share an exclusive three-frame / **120 ms** recipient lease. Different casters and other skills are independent. Create the lease when a qualified area contact is attempted, including a fully immune zero-damage contact. Thus an enemy inside several simultaneous explosions takes the first eligible packet only. At the exact deadline another hit is allowed. The lease is transient; prune expired entries on casts, add no timer/persistence ID, and never queue damage for later replay.
8. **Native recipients and interruptions.** Reuse the existing ordinary copied-area recipient preflight before payment, and recheck recipients during delivery. Unsupported source passive/return/property contexts produce a logged refusal rather than an invented adapter. This prepared proof does not qualify PvP, hirelings or hostile class summons. Apply the ordinary typed spell mitigation, shielding, resistance, immunity, potion and hit-recovery services. If a later callback retires the caster, changes its facet or invalidates a supported context, retain prior mana/life charges, damage and leases; stop/log the remainder without replay or refund.
9. **Persistence.** The cast is instantaneous and its pending windup is transient. It installs no pet buff or field, changes no summon birth stats and creates no new timer, persistence ID or save version. Existing pet damage, RNG, controllers, difficulty rescaling and parking remain unchanged. Saving/loading cannot replay the explosion, repay life, heal the roster or resume a discarded windup. Existing independent projectiles/traps are unaffected.
10. **Assets and verification limit.** Identify the shared `0x36BD` explosion and `0x1E5` cast sound as placeholders. Final `bloodboilexplode`, `warlock_fire_cast_1`, Blood Boil cast/corpse-explosion audio and tree/hotkey icons remain separately tracked. Prepared native checks and original saves verify the proposal; they do not recover do160 or certify every class, property, PvP/global-world/client path.

`HolyBolts` already owns saved straight flights, ability identity distinguishing ordinary and Fist of the Heavens secondary bolts, per-hit exclusive sampling, original missile rank/damage, current Prayer healing synergy, first-collider/LastCollide tracking and ally eligibility. Ordinary Holy Bolt heals allowed players/pets; FoH's parent table has neither TargetAlly nor TargetPet and must not inherit healing merely from a missile flag. Enemy damage is restricted to undead/demons. The pinned Holy1 bit permits positive-resistance bypass for undead; do not invent the same bypass for demons. The historical [evidence](../tools/reference/holy-bolt-evidence.json) retains its existing formula/input/save controls and current-engine limits.

The complete unchanged older `MISSMODE_SrvHit07_HolyBolt_FistOfTheHeavenBolt` now executes in an isolated compiled probe: **768 eligibility,16 healing and four guard contexts**. Missing aliases were restored from the pinned cache after recorded SHA256 and Git-blob checks. See [probe](../tools/probe_holy_bolt_hit_reference.py) and [evidence](../tools/reference/holy-bolt-hit-engine-evidence.json). It verifies short-circuit callback query order, original missile rank/skill passed into both formula slots, exclusive fixed-point random bounds, fallback damage sampling for nonpositive healing, life cap, optional overlay and no healing writes on non-healing branches. Owner/eligibility/species/formula/RNG/stat services are explicitly declared callbacks, not executed engine components.

That old callback's return1/3/4 policy predates modern demon inclusion and friendly/hostile piercing. Preserve current pinned tables and the recorded Blizzard2.4 changes rather than copying old destruction semantics. The older source does not supply modern damage function15. Do not equate a successful callback comparison with proof of the modern function.

Closest current reusable components are `BasicMissileUpdate`, `SourceStepTrace`, shared integer vectors/collision/Slow Missiles capture and source RNG, as used by `SorceressSpellMissiles` and `NecromancerSpellMissiles`. The Sorceress route's class restriction, projectile profiles, cold/fire/lightning packet construction and freeze/armor policy are not Holy Bolt rules. Extract shared transport or add an explicit Paladin route only where contracts match. Preserve per-flight healing rank, current hard Prayer, full piercing and the ordinary-versus-FoH secondary identity. Current double-vector flight, catch-up loop, alive-owner/map/town removal, non-atomic restore and damage receiver are individual migration targets, not already accepted source equivalents.

`SkillCombat.HolyImpact` currently provides fractional direct magic HP and native recovery, with its own resistance-bypass branch; it does not yet use the full ordered spell-to-NPC event/HP/death/return bridge. Existing Sorceress `ImpactPrepared` shares typed mitigation/potion/cold services but also uses direct HP/recovery; do not label either as complete ordered magic contact. Preserve the meaning of the Holy bypass while reviewing flat reduction, absorb, negative resistance, potion additions, missile reaction/avoidance and result/hitclass. The current ordinary cast folds the potion into launch endpoints, while the accepted newer projectile routes check potion eligibility at impact; compare the approved T29 rule before changing this.

Acceptance should retain original healing/damage/rank/hard-synergy/payment/poisoned-ally/piercing/LastCollide/save controls and add actual left/right/quick input, saved source path/seed/slow continuation and independent owner/facet/death/offline cases. Native room/size/terrain/PvP and modern damage function15 remain explicit until supported. Final holy travel/trail/hit/healing/audio and talent/hotkey art stay separate placeholders. No new handler, class completion or deployment is claimed by this preparation.

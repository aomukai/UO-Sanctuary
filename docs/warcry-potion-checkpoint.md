# War Cry and Magical Penetration

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**, with the approved [T29 spell rule](t29-combat-potions.md) taking precedence. The old accepted DLL reproduced the gap on a real release: 100 damage with the potion instead of 140. That baseline was frozen before repair.

New War Cry rings capture elemental bonuses once at release in an optional `ShoutRay.Modifiers` field. Its potion value is always zero: the active potion is read at contact. The existing potion helper adds four noncompounding elements from the original physical damage, and the common mitigation service handles each component once. Added cold and poison cause no extra status. Damage roll, block, stun, payment, timing and geometry retain their existing paths. Health Link receives the settled non-poison components; added poison stays in the primary total.

Only War Cry opts into this payload. Leap Attack's shared physical receiver retains its default and an active-potion negative control confirms its original result. Current production callers do not add a new War Cry item-proc route; named-item admission remains unchanged.

Old v0 rays omit the new field. They retain their physical hit without Magical Penetration. An active-potion contact with no historical capture is explicitly refused before damage or stun and logged; historical bonuses are not invented. Other shouts cannot carry the supplement. Invalid new supplements are rejected before changing any active pool. Profile 140 remains version 0; null fields are omitted from JSON. This is a bounded legacy path, not a claim of every old-binary upgrade working.

# Revive frozen equipment attack rating and defense

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**, approved Sanctuary deviations first. Prepared original Revives now consume frozen equipment attack rating and general defense through the existing NPC stat and hit-check routes. Equipped learned Revive remains gated until the remaining equipment consumers/events are integrated.

An optional `AttackDefenseBases` field extends the existing version-0 before-death payload; absent fields remain absent when serialized. Ordinary native creatures capture their gear-free fallback attack/defense from raw Dexterity after verifying that native Dexterity offsets belong to equipment. The standard native armor getter must be the reviewed Mobile getter, which returns zero; custom Dexterity/armor getters and unrelated native Dexterity modifiers retain explicit unsupported issues. This preserves Sanctuary's existing native combat bases rather than treating UO base attributes as D2 player stats. Existing source bodies use their reviewed source combat getters instead. These tags and captured values are validated; historical records without the field cannot opt into the new component.

The prepared `frozen-equipment-combat-v1` profile composes the previous frozen life/mana/stamina profile. Current authored difficulty attack/defense fields take precedence. A fallback uses the captured gear-free native basis, or the existing source-body getter, so independent loot cannot be counted twice. Frozen equipment contributes flat attack rating plus five times its Dexterity and item attack percentage through `NpcEquipmentCombat` and `ClassStats`. General defense combines captured item defense and a quarter of the total equipment Dexterity; round the pooled Dexterity once, rather than rounding each item. Existing skill/aura/curse percentages remain in their original shared consumers. Bound immutable death contexts are cached; no item object is reconstructed or consulted after death.

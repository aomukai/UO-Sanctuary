# Native armor-ignore shield producer

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**, approved Sanctuary deviations first. This corrects a missing native damage producer, reusing existing shield formulas and the established Cyclone → Energy → Bone → Ward host pipeline. It introduces no new shield priority policy or source drain interpretation. **Charms and runewords are excluded.**

**Native1/C1/I1/source2/run1 passes33 exact results, six actual Energy Shield casts, six connections, three saves and two reloads.** [Driver](../tests/check_unresisted_shields.py), [receipt](../tools/reference/unresisted-shields-native-evidence.json), [recorder](../tools/record_unresisted_shields.py). Native/content/integration builds have zero warnings/errors. No unsupported-context warnings, save-deletion warnings or connection denials occur. Installed protection passes for 106 artifacts, 38 saves, 20 configurations and 44 client patches.

The old native armor-ignore hook supplied only total/physical data and entered the finite physical pool helper. Energy Shield alone did not activate that path; its mana ledger was never called. Patch [0075](../patches/ModernUO/0075-typed-unresisted-shield-packet.patch) adds an immutable, nonpersisted native packet with physical/fire/cold/poison/lightning percentages and direct/weapon/ranged context after the existing chaos allocation. The new typed callback is tried before the unchanged legacy callback. Only normalized, classified packets with active Energy Shield or Cyclone Armor enter the new path; unshielded/Bone/Ward-only callers retain the legacy path.

`SkillCombat` shares its existing bound-physical preparation, Ward ticket, spectral addition and result delivery between both branches. Qualified typed input now passes through `DefensiveArmors.Absorb`, preserving the existing mana calculation/live hard Telekinesis, finite pools and poison exclusion. Native armor-ignore still bypasses resistance. The old producer's placement, native extra modifiers and unsupported exceptional/PvP ordering are not recertified by this correction. Existing shield casting, saved formats and effect/icon placeholders remain intact.

Native controls verify Energy Shield on physical and mixed five-field packets; poison does not consume mana. Actual Shaftstop proves armor-ignore bypasses its resistance while an ordinary resisted hit still uses it. One-point fractional damage, low-mana partial absorption, depletion, physical-first legacy Energy-before-Bone order and the Druid's existing Cyclone elemental pool pass. Unknown native direct/remainder projections and injected Energy/Ward overlaps refuse before changing life, mana or shield pools. These contexts remain unsupported instead of being guessed. No monster life/mana/stamina drain fields are fabricated from poison or leech.

The original saved Sorceress, NPC attacker, Shaftstop, ranked shield/deadline, fractional wounds and mana retain across both reloads. Each resumed native mixed hit updates the existing wounds and mana without refilling, recasting or extending the shield. Existing Energy Shield source/cast controls use their cached cases, and real right/quick casts and Bone Armor/encounter regressions remain. No unchanged reference compiler matrix or whole class cohort was rerun.

Only two `Projects/UOContent` files change; **ModernUO.dll and Server.dll retain their accepted hashes**, and no server-engine source or saved format changes. The installed server is untouched.

Four Warlock proposal pairs remain frozen pending. Historical receipts keep their own preserved source/artifact identity and are not regenerated.

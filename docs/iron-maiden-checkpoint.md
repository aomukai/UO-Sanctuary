# Iron Maiden checkpoint

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**; approved Sanctuary deviations take precedence. This isolated candidate preserves the installed build and existing saves. No class completion is claimed.

Iron Maiden now uses the shared learned Necromancer do30/filter3 curse cast and recipient path. Radius is seven source subtiles under the existing world mapping; mana is five once at release, including a valid empty/resisted area. Duration is `300 + 60 × (rank − 1)` frames: 12 seconds at rank 1 and 60 seconds at effective rank 21. Weaker same-skill recasts reject, equal ranks retain the original creator and refresh expiry, and higher/different curses replace. Attract and the shared possessed/species/attackability rules remain enforced. There are no aura-stat slots and no invented resistance payload.

Reflection now consumes the **fractional physical damage event before victim-life clipping and defender Life Tap**, replacing the old whole-HP after-hit calculation in supported native and prepared paths. Ordinary monsters use `200 + 25 × (rank − 1)` percent; the player/hireling branch uses the quarter formula. The shared source percentage helper retains its observable large-value early division. Unrepresentable signed intermediate arithmetic explicitly refuses/logs rather than silently wrapping or inventing a saturated result.

Native/prepared attacker events deliver reflection once; later compatibility hooks explicitly suppress duplicates. Direct whole-HP compatibility calls still enter the same return primitive, without claiming to supply a full source event. Ranged, zero-physical and nonweapon spell paths do not acquire melee reflection. Reflection uses existing physical mitigation/health and does not add weapon damage, mastery, potion elements or recursive weapon callbacks. Shared retaliation now includes the existing prepared Psychic Ward physical pool as well as Bone Armor and resistance; its reactive event37 remains gated.

Reflected death does not cancel the original qualified damage packet. The prepared pipeline captures the responsible pet owner before damage events, retains that owner through a reflection-induced pet retirement, and restores the native responsible-damage sub-entry when retirement has cleared the master. It registers the original hit once and retains owner attribution through deferred recipient death. This state is scoped to the synchronous packet and never serialized or recovered from unrelated damage history. Native-only legacy pet bridges keep their broader lifecycle/credit limitations.

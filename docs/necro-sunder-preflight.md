# Necromancer Sunder review

The mechanical pin remains **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. The approved Sanctuary policy captures summon inputs when summoned. Nonzero Sunder delivery remains unsupported; this review does not enable it or reinterpret its numeric value as ordinary resistance piercing.

Current owned tables put `300` on each elemental, physical and magic Sunder charm. The corresponding stat IDs are cold187, fire189, lightning190, poison191, physical192 and magic193. Revive's six passive formulas copy those owner stats directly. Other Necromancer summon rows copy their relevant channels. Existing birth adapters explicitly reject nonzero values; Iron Golem also has a live-owner rejection in contact preflight. That live read needs review against the approved summon snapshot rule when a supported nonzero source exists.

[Blizzard's original Sunder announcement](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23827590/diablo-ii-resurrected-ladder-season-two-has-concluded) documents an immune non-player resistance result of 95%, before other resistance reductions, and extension to pets and summons. It is historical documentation, not a captured 3.3.93847 reducer. Its broad wording does not prove how the stored300 value behaves for extreme resistances, stacked sources or every special recipient. The user-supplied [mechanics site](https://diabloforfans.com/mechanics) is useful corroborating material, but its reviewed page does not establish that numeric consumer either.

The current `NamedElements.Resistance` path has no Sunder step. It already composes original resistance, native/managed Conviction, captured curse deltas, Cold Mastery, item piercing and Sanctuary. The prepared NPC weapon path and Mage direct hits use that service; Creeper poison uses it with no player gear. A complete implementation must preserve the original immunity basis for reduced curse/aura effectiveness, source identity, captured inputs and already-fired packets. Simply subtracting300 or unconditionally rewriting resistance would not establish those requirements.

`NamedGear.Load` explicitly excludes charm bases `cm1/cm2/cm3`, and current item-audit tests enforce that policy. This review does not change acquisition, add an invented equipment source, or use a fixture to claim that charms are playable. Charm policy and the Sunder reducer are separate questions.

Next Sunder work: obtain a verified current reducer contract or explicitly approved adaptation; bind captured channels to each summon and its issued packets; test immune/nonimmune and high-resistance boundaries, curses/Conviction, item piercing, owner gear changes, caster removal, facet rescaling and saves. Keep other skills moving meanwhile. The running D2R client is unnecessary for this table/code review, and no game process was read or controlled.

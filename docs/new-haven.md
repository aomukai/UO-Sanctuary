# New Haven

New characters arrive in New Haven. The harbour guard points them north to the Bountiful Harvest Inn, where Iolo explains why he summoned the Avatar. Their first meeting adds one entry to the journal. Iolo offers to join when the character has reached level 6. Every starting class receives a blessed dagger that grants one tier-one skill while equipped.

| Area | Monster levels | Encounters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graveyard northeast of town | 1–3 | Zombies and skeletons |
| South farm beyond Old Haven | 4–6 | Bandits around the farm road, campfire, and barn |
| Old Haven ruins | 4–6 | Skeletons and zombies among the streets |
| New Haven Mine | 6–8 | Earth elementals in the upper galleries |
| Below the mine's rope | 8–10 | Lizardmen and Boss Krulg, a level 10 ogre |

The graveyard, ruins and mine are open to exploration in any order. The mountain entrance south of town at (3508, 2777–2778) opens into the upper galleries. Garran and Bess warn travellers about the mine at the campfire on the approach. Iolo can chime in if he is travelling with the Avatar. You can also walk up to the black shaft or use the signed stone landing on the road toward Old Haven. Earth elementals occupy both sides of the upper loop. Below, small lizardman groups roam away from the rope landing, and Krulg occupies the northern chamber.

Double-click the raised rope handles to climb between galleries, even when a corpse is on the landing.

Pets and active summons follow through mine entrances, ropes, moongates, and player teleports. Summons wait outside when the Avatar enters a guarded town.

Surface encounters have twice their base life (players-3). Upper and lower mine encounters have triple their base life (players-5). These settings affect life only. Each kill grants its existing experience reward.

Old Haven’s undead are spread along the northern approach, streets, and southern ruins. Rabbits, birds, and deer populate the woods and hills around both towns. Old Haven’s furnishings and working doors come from a curated set of the stock Haven decoration files.

Merryn offers free resurrection at the signed healer’s house northwest of the arrival point, past the bank. Elin points ghosts toward the house when they come within speaking range. If Iolo falls, his pale spirit follows the Avatar. Bring him to Merryn out of combat and click her to ask her to call him back, free of charge.

Island enemies use the Normal Act I treasure classes, with a chance of no drop, gold, consumables, or gear. Krulg rolls one random Normal modifier when he spawns. He uses two boosted gear rolls, three potion rolls and a guaranteed gold drop, then respawns after five to seven minutes. The island uses five times the normal archetype XP as a first tuning pass for the existing level curve.

Click a trainer to buy supplies or pay for lessons. Training costs 1 gold per 0.1 skill up to 30.0. Choosing a first craft requires confirmation. Gathering skills remain available alongside the chosen craft. The lumberjack stocks hatchets and the miner stocks picks.

Elara the Scholar works in the north mage tower alongside the alchemy and inscription trainers. Elara identifies equipment for 50 gold per item and sells Identify and Town Portal scrolls.

Speaking to Iolo once the Avatar has reached level 6 permanently unlocks outbound travel through the island's public moongate. The player can travel alone or invite him along. Until that conversation, the gate points the player back to Iolo at the inn.

The public moongate's world selector currently offers Britannia. Its towns and countryside use the ordinary animal entries from ModernUO's stock spawn data. New Haven uses its own population.

Iolo offers to wait at the inn if the Avatar chooses to travel alone. His farewell points them to the bard’s college in Britain once they leave. The first successful trip through New Haven’s moongate closes recruitment and removes the inn’s Iolo for that character. If Iolo travels with the Avatar, he speaks after they arrive. He suggests seeing Lord British and talks about passing his songs on to the young bards at the college.

When Iolo joins the Avatar, his Graveyard topic disappears. After the Avatar explores the mine and defeats Krulg, Iolo’s Danger topic connects the mine to reports from across Britannia. The Ogre topic lets him reflect on Krulg’s unfamiliar powers based on what he witnessed or what the Avatar told him.

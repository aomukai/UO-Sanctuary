# New Haven

New characters arrive in New Haven. The harbour guard points them north to the Bountiful Harvest Inn, where Iolo explains why he summoned the Avatar. Their first meeting adds one entry to the journal. Iolo offers to join when the character has reached level 6. Every starting class receives a blessed dagger that grants one tier-one skill while equipped.

| Area | Monster levels | Encounters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graveyard northeast of town | 1–3 | Zombies and skeletons |
| South farm beyond Old Haven | 4–6 | Bandits around the farm road, campfire, and barn |
| Old Haven ruins | 4–6 | Skeletons and zombies among the streets |
| New Haven Mine | 6–8 | Earth elementals in the upper galleries |
| Below the mine's rope | 8–10 | Lizardmen and Boss Krulg, a level 10 ogre |

The graveyard, ruins and mine are open to exploration in any order. A signed entrance on the road toward Old Haven leads into the mine. Double-clicking the rope takes the player between the upper and lower galleries.

Old Haven’s undead are spread along the northern approach, streets, and southern ruins. Rabbits, birds, and deer populate the woods and hills around both towns. Old Haven’s furnishings and working doors come from a curated set of the stock Haven decoration files.

Merryn offers free resurrection at the signed healer’s house northwest of the arrival point, past the bank. Elin points ghosts toward the house when they come within speaking range.

Island enemies use the Normal Act I treasure classes, with a chance of no drop, gold, consumables, or gear. Krulg uses two boosted gear rolls, three potion rolls and a guaranteed gold drop, then respawns after five to seven minutes. The island uses five times the normal archetype XP as a first tuning pass for the existing level curve.

The market's existing instructors teach trades. The lumberjack stocks hatchets and the miner stocks picks. Lumberjacking and Mining are available alongside the character's chosen craft.

Speaking to Iolo once the Avatar has reached level 6 permanently unlocks outbound travel through the island's public moongate. The player can travel alone or invite him along. Until that conversation, the gate points the player back to Iolo at the inn.

Iolo offers to wait at the inn if the Avatar chooses to travel alone. His farewell points them to the bard’s college in Britain once they leave. The first successful trip through New Haven’s moongate closes recruitment and removes the inn’s Iolo for that character. Iolo travels with the Avatar if he already joined.

# T14 — recognition and earned wardrobe access

Scope: permanent, per-character recognition from class level80 or any crafting skill100. Gathering does not qualify; Cartography does. Eligible characters receive a town-hall invitation. Recognition confers wardrobe/housing rights and named painting certificates; the first completed ceremony also awards one bookshelf wardrobe. Rights permit acquiring a house, without granting a free one. Craft switching is delivered by [T15](t15-wardrobe.md); actual housing acquisition/placement remains T42.

User decisions confirmed20 October2026 (JST): rights survive lost/sold furniture or housing; ceremonies can be completed at any supported town hall; both honors can be earned separately, with one certificate each and furniture only once. Hero of the People uses the combat route; Pillar of Society uses the crafting route. These are stored honors, without changing the existing level/class display or deciding the later selectable-title catalogue.

Acceptance checks:

- Exactly80 class level or100.0 base crafting skill qualifies, without bonuses or gathering. All ten crafts, including Cartography, work. Existing eligible characters are detected on login/interaction without changing their progression.
- Invitations and eligibility persist. Both honors and all delivered/pending reward claims survive reload. A character's rights survive skill/furniture/house changes and are not inherited by another character on the account.
- Ceremony responses revalidate character, connection, hall identity, proximity/line of sight and current award state. Cancellation, repeated/stale/remote responses cannot duplicate rewards.
- A full or overweight backpack leaves individual awards pending for later collection, without dropping them in the world or losing earned rights. Retries may finish a partly delivered set; lost/sold already-delivered rewards are not automatically reissued.
- Supported civic services persist without duplicate registrars/public wardrobes. Furniture is a character-owned access point, not storage for progression. Public access remains available before home ownership.
- Verify the real ceremony interface and both eligibility paths, capacity failures and save/reload, then deploy with a full backup and preservation of current characters/configuration/client preferences.

The second completed ceremony, whichever route is earned last, also unlocks active-class respeccing for5,000 gold. Reset all allocated class skill/stat points, retaining the active class, level, XP and every native trade skill/cap/lock. No inactive profile can be selected. Successful reset closes the wardrobe and confirmation and invalidates the wardrobe session; it cannot be combined with a switch. T15 must retain that session validation.

Respec uses a nearby registered public wardrobe or the recipient's placed wardrobe in an owned home. A carried furniture award is not portable respec access. Access requires alive, ready and out of combat: no target/cast/action/recovery deadline, no fighting follower and no aggression within10seconds. Pending native targeting/casting must finish or be cancelled. The character pays from backpack gold when it covers the full cost, otherwise from bank gold; it does not combine partial balances. Full costs and confirmation appear before charge. No allocated points means no charge. Current pools are capped to their final maximums without refilling, effects/debuffs and action deadlines remain, class summons are dismissed, and real gear that no longer meets requirements stays safely in the backpack even when it exceeds capacity. Ordinary pets remain.

Supported services:22 civic offices across11 towns on Felucca and Trammel. Minoc and Skara Brae use their native town-hall signs; Britain, Jhelom, Magincia, Moonglow, Nujel'm, Serpent's Hold, Trinsic, Vesper and Yew use native Counselor's Guild buildings as civic recognition offices. `recognition-halls.json` records the pinned native sign anchors. Actual NPC/wardrobe floor placement is validated and saved. A public wardrobe also opens recognition/status before the unlock; T15 adds the earned craft selector.

`SanctuaryRecognition` version0 stores eligibility, completed honors, delivered certificates, the one furniture claim and service identities. Eligibility latches when a milestone is reached and is rechecked on login/interaction for existing characters. Furniture holds recipient metadata, without owning progression. Native patch0024 adds only an optional PlayerMobile skill-change notification; the engine/client are unchanged.

Status: implemented, tested and deployed20 October2026 (JST). The live server is running with recognition/respec enabled.

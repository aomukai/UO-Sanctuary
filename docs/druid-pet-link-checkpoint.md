# Druid pet damage and Health Link

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**, approved Sanctuary deviations first. This connects current pet attacks to the approved [Health Link contract](defiler-health-link-rule-proposal.md).

The accepted parent DLL reproduced the missing-components fault on a real Spirit Wolf attack: full primary damage of 921 FP256, but linked damage of 0 instead of 488. The outcome and source RNG were correct; the event carried no typed components. That baseline was frozen before gameplay edits.

Spirit Wolf and Dire Wolf now publish their existing settled damage components through typed health delivery. Spirit Wolf retains cold-only intrinsic damage with supported external elements; no physical minimum is added. Grizzly publishes its cached four-channel health total. Its committed physical component is that total minus the unchanged fire, cold and lightning components. BearSmite's later physical-field addition is excluded from health loss and sharing, while existing leech, retaliation and bookkeeping continue to use their original fields.

No damage formula, critical chance, hit/block roll, cold effect, upgrade, Crushing Blow, Open Wounds, stun, knockback, regeneration, snapshot, AI or resource rule changes. No saved field or version is added. Actual pet identity remains the root attacker; native damage responsibility still points to its Druid owner. Health Link's secondary packets retain Defiler attribution and suppressed recursion.

The matrix has **32 cases per boot**: Spirit Wolf, Dire Wolf, ordinary Grizzly and BearSmite, each with normal, Enchant, secondary resistance, primary resistance, immunity, critical, miss and shield-block contexts. Checks cover exact primary/secondary FP256 loss, settled channels, one nonrecursive root event or no event for zero damage, deterministic source RNG, native owner-credit entries and no extra owner mana payment. BearSmite explicitly retains the gap between its cached health total and later physical field.

Cases use actual prepared pets, captured mode stats and the real hit/prepare/release services. Their temporary skeleton recipient supports the existing shield component routine. The original Defiler group, source shields, target position and knockback states are restored after the scene. The new contact matrix does not execute AI scheduling; the retained complete Druid cohort covers source packets, AI, sequences, upgrades, casts, snapshots, parking and original saved actors.

**Content 1 / Integration 7 / source 7 / native run 1 passed 684 exact results, 204 connections, 156 ordinary input actions, nine held-Arctic sequences, three saves and two reloads.** Both accepted builds have zero warnings/errors, and the first corrected gameplay run passed. Three fixture compile failures were frozen: the native credit ledger needed its Server.Collections iteration extension. The first baseline attempt used a zombie without shield variants and was also frozen before isolating the skeleton receiver; a separate second baseline then proved the old-DLL damage fault. The accepted transcript retains five WardrobeSet save-pruning warnings and six deliberate scalar-link refusals (two per boot). This is not a warning-free save certificate.

Only remaining-description text changes for the three affected talents. All 240 handlers, numerical tables, completed-class count and 573 named-definition outcomes retain. There is no new save field/version, native/client build or asset. Installed artifacts, saves, configuration and client patches remain protected. Effects/audio and talent/hotkey icons remain in their separate placeholder registers.

This checkpoint does not certify the class, exact modern engine ordering, special/PvP recipients, graphical behavior or historical binary upgrades. Other scalar/legacy producers and the separately documented open decisions remain unaffected.

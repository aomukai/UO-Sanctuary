Blade Warp is implemented as an explicitly authorized Sanctuary estimate against **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. This permission covers Blade Warp; Mirrored Blades remains a separate unfinished skill.

The [current-build extractor](https://diabloforfans.com/skill/blade-warp) describes an astral weapon followed by a teleport to its impact. Owned skill390 and missile707/visual692 are retained locally. Their inputs supply an ordinary SC cast, melee-weapon requirement, 15 mana, 20-frame cooldown, velocity36, 20 missile updates, four source units of blast radius, magic damage and 24% hard-point synergies from Echoing Strike/Mirrored Blades. The skill has no weapon-source damage byte. The implementation reuses the existing Warlock clock, action session, projectile vector/subcell crossing, collision hitboxes, magic/potion mitigation, cooldown store and teleport/arrival service.

The chosen Sanctuary behavior is one blade aimed at the released cursor or selected enemy position. It ends at that point, first hostile contact, wall or lifetime limit. One two-tile magic blast occurs before teleport; there is no additional direct weapon hit, attack roll, critical, leech, durability loss or on-strike proc. Release rank, hard synergies, magic enhancement and potion modifiers are frozen in the flight. The impact uses existing defender mitigation and avoidance. Landing tries the impact tile, then its eight neighbors in distance/X/Y order, with existing floor, fit, LOS, house, travel and town checks. No valid landing leaves the caster in place without refunding a released spell.

Windup uses shared Stop, movement, equipment and late-mana cancellation. Allocation failure publishes no flight and pays nothing; successful release pays exactly once and starts the existing saved 0.8-second cooldown. Subsequent gear changes do not rewrite or cancel the released blade. Death, logout, facet travel or entering a safe area cancel travel; Stop after release does not recall it. The short mobility flight is deliberately transient across process restart, preventing deferred offline teleports. This adds no persistence ID or new save format; existing shared cooldown persistence remains intact.

# Hex: Purge reaction proposal

This is an **unapproved Sanctuary adaptation** for the unavailable recipient/attachment callbacks in **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. The pinned skill389 table supplies damage, synergies, attack bonuses, duration, chance, radius and charge count. It does not establish the current event ordering, reapplication behavior, mastery placement or complete incoming-event coverage. Learned Purge and automatic weapon/recipient hooks remain disabled.

The proposed complete rule is:

- A qualified landed weapon event, or an explicitly qualified Hex trigger, attaches a mark. Misses, block/dodge and unrelated spells do not attach one. An otherwise landed hit may attach even if resistance prevents life loss; only positive direct damage can trigger an explosion. Install the mark before the applying hit reaches the shared health notification, so that hit can trigger it.
- Capture the registered Purge rank and the hard synergies, magic mastery and Magical Penetration modifiers at attachment. Keep that snapshot for the mark's lifetime. Apply magic mastery once to its FP256 explosion endpoints before rolling; the explosion receives no second weapon-mastery pass. Potion additions use their captured elemental masteries and each recipient's current independent defenses.
- Use the pinned chance, clamped to 0–100%, against an unsigned 0–99 roll. A failed roll advances the mark's saved random stream but spends no charge. A success spends one charge before any damage or death callbacks. Start with one charge, two at rank10 and three at rank20 or above. Remove an exhausted mark; a later qualified weapon event can attach a fresh one. Use the shared source RNG/range roller with a saved stream per mark; global D2R caster-stream interleaving is not claimed.
- An equal-rank hit from the same caster refreshes the original duration without refilling charges, rerolling the stream or rewriting captured stats. An equal-rank mark from another caster and any weaker-rank replacement are refused. A stronger rank replaces the mark, caster and snapshot with a fresh charge budget. A successful different recipient Hex uses the already-approved group replacement; failed attachment preserves the existing Hex. Changing the caster's weapon buff does not remove an existing recipient mark.
- React to positive post-mitigation direct HP packets, including fractional and lethal ones, from any source. Poison/periodic damage, returned damage, life costs, zero or fully mitigated packets and event-suppressed secondary packets do not trigger a burst. Native ordinary-NPC incoming damage, including armor-ignore delivery, opts into the existing fixed-point health bridge while a prepared reactive mark is present.
- Roll one magic packet per explosion and apply it to eligible hostile NPCs in the inclusive two-tile circle around the marked victim, using the shared area/height/safety filters and LOS from that victim. Include the victim and credit explosion damage to the mark's caster. Use current defenses, without weapon accuracy, shield block, critical, leech, hit recovery or weapon/kill procs from this secondary packet. The explosion cannot trigger another Purge mark. Missile717 is visual only and does not deal a second hit.
- Reactions require a living Warlock/Witch owner who is online and in the recipient's facet. Logout, facet separation and owner death suspend reactions while the original expiry continues; returning before expiry can resume them. The recipient must remain hostile and alive at notification. A lethal incoming hit still explodes before life is committed. Unsupported special receivers return an explicit fault before charges, seed or explosion HP change; they are not silently treated as ordinary NPCs.

The implementation is a prepared controller, not a learned skill. It reuses the existing HexPurge persistence185/version0 and raw plan, approved Hex groups, SourceRandom/SourceSpellPacketRoll, SourceDamageAreas, shared modifier/potion/mitigation services, FP256 health and pair-scoped event suppression. Optional `Reaction` metadata stores remaining charges, captured mastery/modifiers and the stream. Old-schema JSON omits that field and remains a nonreactive prepared plan. No automatic upgrade, new timer or persistence identity is added. Invalid snapshots are rejected atomically.

The focused fixture passes **15 native markers across three boots, two reloads and three saves**, together with the existing direct-damage bridge regression. It checks chance77/78 and the100% cap, same-caster refresh without refilling, weaker/equal-other/stronger competition, failed/successful recipient-group handling, inclusive radius/diagonal/height/ally/LOS filtering, shared packets, mastery/potion arithmetic, real native NPC and armor-ignore contacts, fractional damage, zero/immunity/periodic/return/suppressed exclusions, offline/facet/dead-owner suspension, buff/recipient independence, natural expiry, recursion suppression and lethal centers. Original actors retain a genuinely spent two-charge budget, advanced random stream, captured modifiers, equipment and a129/256 wound across reloads; the next saved-stream decision is checked before another control is created. Old-schema JSON omission and malformed-snapshot atomicity also pass.

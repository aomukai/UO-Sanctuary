**Accepted: C4/I4/S7/N4; 33 native markers, twelve authenticated connections, three boots/saves and two reloads.**

Reference remains **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. This composes the existing original-body host with a prepared chance evaluator and transient channel. **It does not enable learned Bind Demon or constitute a complete approved adaptation.** Coverage stays **226/240 handlers, Warlock 17/30; no class certified**.

The [rule review](../tools/reference/bind-demon-channel-rule-review.json) distinguishes retained data from the proposed decisions. All 171 species keep their raw strict health thresholds, hard-point gates, chance skill term and max-life supplier. Existing `SkillFormula` gains remainder syntax with multiplicative precedence and inactive-branch safety. Only this explicit captured-caster adapter maps `sksrc`; no general source-skill lookup meaning is invented.

The ordinary health term's ambiguous `elte==1+elte==4` is explicitly grouped as `(elte==1)+(elte==4)`. That proposes divisors 20 for qualities 1/4, 10 for quality 2, and 5 otherwise. It deliberately differs from the prior compiler-port observation and is **not claimed as recovered game behavior**. The numerical quality contexts are not a new mapping for native UO champions or encounters.

The prepared controller requires a trained, living, connected Warlock, an eligible original body and at least nine mana. Press is free; first attempt occurs after one second. Each attempt costs a proposed **fixed nine mana at all ranks**, including failed rolls, and supplies progress 12 to the retained modulo gate. This does not derive a current SQ6/progress/payment cadence from the raw shifted table costs (200/256 at rank 1, 276/256 at rank 20). Those choices must appear in the complete proposal before admission. Positive chance uses one roll from 0–99; zero chance draws none. A late cancellation keeps the released attempt's debit.

The original target stays locked. Qualification checks actual same-facet/range/LOS, demon identity, native controller/typed damage, species, life baseline and source suppliers. It has no ownership, saved-baseline or aura-RNG mutation. Successful binding requalifies and uses the existing original-body transaction; only then is aura choice captured. Ordinary target AI is not stopped during the channel. `MonsterVariantBaselines.PreviewNormal` supplies the existing Normal calculation without committing it before success.

Movement, weapon changes, control/hit-recovery/spell interruptions, class/connection/target/range/LOS loss or an expired held lease stop further work. Existing 750-ms transport leases renew without independent payment. There is no missed-attempt replay after lag, duplicate press or repeated deadline. Cast identity and attempt generation protect against stale callbacks, including a renewal callback starting a fresh channel and a reentrant newer attempt. Held intent is transient across logout/restart. No production timer or automatic input/world scheduling is added by this prepared component.

Focused native checks cover all 171 threshold/off-cycle/chance/max-HP contexts; named hard-20 and ordinary hard-10/15 gates; free press/windup/heartbeat; failed, empty-mana and successful attempts; no catch-up; movement/paralysis/range/lease cancellation; invalid RNG; stale/reentrant callbacks; actual binding with original wounds and exactly one aura draw; and saved original snapshots/resources with no restored held intent.

The existing prepared Ward supplies its pinned capacity, hard-point synergies, mana, captured rank/stun duration, physical pool, group-1 replacement and persistence. The new candidate proposes:

- Capture the active Ward once when a **successful hostile NPC melee contact with positive pre-absorption physical damage** is established. Misses, blocks/dodges, ranged attacks, purely elemental hits and zero-physical hits do not capture a reaction.
- Consume its physical pool before physical resistance, using the existing fractional min(pool, damage) operation. An exact depletion removes the Ward; overflow continues to mitigation and life loss.
- Retain that hit's captured registration even when the pool fully absorbs the hit or is exhausted by it. Deliver the reaction once in the shared incoming-melee path, after attacker Iron Maiden/lightning-return processing and before Clay Golem slow, Life Tap, leech, Thorns and incoming life loss. A dead attacker or invalid recipient receives no stun. A consumed ticket cannot refresh the effect or be reused for another recipient.
- Use the existing shared stun rules and the Ward's captured duration: normal rank-1 duration is 44 frames; existing unique/boss/velocity restrictions still apply. This proposal adds no independent boss-stun chance, life-loss prerequisite or extra mana cost.
- Cast on self through the existing ordinary Warlock SC clock. Windup is unpaid; release uses the current effective rank and table cost once (20 mana at rank 1). Stop, movement, incapacitation, weapon change or release-time mana loss prevents the release. Allocation failure retains the existing do18 ordering: clear the armor group, fail without payment. A successful recast replaces its group and refills the new pool.
- Keep existing death/respec/history removal, persistent fractional pool and maximum, and group-1 armor replacement. No new timer, saved hit ticket, offline healing or reset is introduced.

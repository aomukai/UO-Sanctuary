# Social encounters and class recognition

Small social pockets let the Avatar meet named inhabitants in otherwise hostile places. Class can give someone a reason to welcome you. Acquaintance belongs to the character and can persist across a wardrobe change. The server owns friendships, access and services, and keeps those separate from the current state of a shared room. ## Blood Dungeon entrance

The entrance imps are a shared encounter. During an unclaimed entrance or respawn, the server can establish a peaceful phase if an eligible Warlock, Witch, or known acquaintance is present. An unrecognised Avatar who enters first can start an ordinary hostile encounter. The next room is hostile for everyone.

| Situation | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| A Paladin starts fighting the imps and a Warlock arrives | The fight continues. The imps and eligible Warlock are unable to attack each other, and private conversation is unavailable during the fight. |
| The imps respawn with the Warlock still there | The new encounter can begin pacified. The social group is protected from attacks by all players. Eligible characters can talk; outsiders can receive unfriendly flavor such as a snarl. |
| The Warlock departs and the Paladin who was present during pacification stays | The Paladin’s presence preserves the current peaceful encounter. |
| The last participant preserving the peaceful phase leaves or logs out | Release the encounter and let the next arrival establish its state. |

Store encounter mode and current participants as temporary server state. Durable acquaintance belongs in the character save. Switch modes at encounter boundaries, and protect ongoing conversations from conflicting arrivals. Resolve room bounds, eligibility when several players enter together, death, logout, and respawn before implementing. If state is ambiguous, favor holding the current peace until a safe reset.

## Personal discoveries and recruits

An authored map can reveal a concealed door on an earlier dungeon floor. A rescued captive might appear later at an inn and become recruitable, with that Avatar seeing the now-empty cage when they return. Both can work through per-character state alongside another player’s version of the discovery. Sailors could introduce the Avatar to Buccaneer’s Den, with the inn as a place to visit and hear stories. Campaign transport to Hell’s mainland has its own rules. [The invasion campaign](invasion-campaign.md) · [The Avatar’s journal and virtues](journal-and-virtues.md)

A pirate at a dice table in Buccaneer’s Den is a candidate companion. The player can click the dice to play. The game records the first meeting, completed games, and recruitment eligibility. Three games, seven days since meeting, and an offer on about one in ten eligible interactions are playtest ideas. A broken leg that heals over time is another story idea. The player accepts a current offer through an explicit choice. The pirate can later recognise someone the Avatar meets and open an authored conversation.

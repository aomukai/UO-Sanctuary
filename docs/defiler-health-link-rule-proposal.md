# Defiler Health Link: proposed Sanctuary rule

Status: **proposal; not approved or enabled**. Reference is D2R Reign of the Warlock3.3.93847. The owned NPC405 row,start70/do162/event38 and direct user observations are retained. The saved current do162 is protected;its complete execution and damage-binding behavior are unavailable. No closed-client capture is being retried.

The user-provided [Defiler guide](https://diabloforfans.com/skill/summon-defiler),which identifies the same game version,corroborates separate per-Defiler groups,20-second marks that survive leaving range,and immediate sharing that can amplify separate hits of one area attack. It does not establish the exact packet stage,recipient defenses,overlap/credit rules or callback caps. Those are proposed explicitly below.

1. Defilers remain support-only Gazer summons. They never gain a weapon attack. Each owns its own marked group;marks use a separate state from curses.
2. Use the pinned25-source-cell radius and500-frame lifetime. A source-selected enemy is first,then other eligible hostile NPCs in radius,ordered by distance and serial. Existing friendly,safe-area,facet,height and LOS rules apply. Bosses use ordinary link eligibility unless an explicit source exclusion is established.
3. A cast adds up to `min(5,Health Link rank+1)` new marks,within that Defiler's capacity. Existing marks are not overwritten or refreshed. A monster belongs to one group;an already-marked monster is skipped. Capacity full means no new cast.
4. Capture own effective NPC ranks at cast release through the existing innate getter. Owner gear does not rewrite a living summon. Use the exact table curves,then explicitly cap sharing at60% and capacity at5. Rank25 remains55%/5. The uncapped raw expressions reach61%/6 at99;the runtime caps are a proposed adaptation rather than hidden parser repair.
5. A mark remains until its original20-second expiry even if its target leaves the application radius. Every spread still requires living,hostile recipients and the active owner/Defiler on the same facet. Parking/logout suppresses sharing while deadlines continue. Defiler/owner death or deletion removes its group;no recovery or timer reset occurs.
6. Preserve the original hit in full. Once that hit's mitigation and shields are settled,but before life commitment,copy the pinned fraction of each positive physical,magic,fire,cold and lightning component to **each other** marked monster. Round each component down in FP8. Use the original calculated hit,including overkill,rather than clamping to remaining life. A zero-damage hit creates no share.
7. Direct hits and separate area-hit recipients can each initiate sharing. Periodic poison/other damage-over-time ticks do not initiate sharing under this rule. Sharing is immediate queued damage,not a new damage-over-time effect.
8. Each recipient applies its own ordinary flat reduction,resistances,immunities and shields to the copied typed packet. No new accuracy,shield-block or dodge roll occurs. The Defiler's unverified ordinary-damage multiplier,weapon stats and the owner's potion/mastery bonuses are not applied again.
9. The Defiler is the secondary attacker and its owner receives credit through the existing pet-credit service. Shared packets cannot initiate another link spread,critical/crushing/open-wounds/leech or on-hit/on-strike effects. Ordinary recovery/death and existing on-death observers still work. Use the existing ordered damage and FIFO processing services.
10. Link state,creator,group membership,captured rank/percent and absolute expiry use validated saved records. Reload retains original deadlines and admits no offline damage. Malformed ownership or unsupported contexts refuse explicitly. Visual link markers remain identified placeholders;icons/effects stay in their separate registers.

Approval would authorize these unresolved edges as Sanctuary adaptations. It would not certify current D2R callback equivalence. The numeric suppliers and shared damage/receiver primitives can be prepared independently;automatic linking and the learned Defiler route remain gated until the behavior is settled and verified.

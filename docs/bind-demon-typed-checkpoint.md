# Bind Demon typed native damage

Prepared component against **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**, with approved Sanctuary deviations taking precedence. This extends the [original living-body host](bind-demon-host-checkpoint.md); it does not complete or admit Bind Demon. Coverage remains **226/240 handlers, Warlock 17/30; no class certified**. The native Imp's original melee split is 0% physical, 50% fire and 50% poison. Adding Bind's flat damage to its native damage endpoints incorrectly distributes that addition into fire and poison. The prepared adapter now preserves the base endpoints and adds captured flat damage to the physical component of a positive native weapon packet, before the existing physical-percent pool. Native elemental components and nonweapon spell packets retain their own delivery. This is an explicit prepared UO projection, **not recovered current D2R binder ordering**. Normalized physical/fire/cold/poison/energy splits are supported by the adapter; chaos, direct or malformed splits still refuse before ownership mutation. Existing source-row and controller qualification remains mandatory.

A native 100-point 0/50/50 weapon packet therefore delivers 292.5 physical plus 50 fire and 50 poison against zero resistance. A nonweapon packet remains 50 fire and 50 poison. A 20/80 physical/fire packet delivers 370.5 physical and 80 fire. Each component still uses its own mitigation. Native armor-ignore retains its existing UO bypass semantics; this does not certify D2 special-ability parity. A zero native base packet creates no flat damage.

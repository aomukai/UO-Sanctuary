# Prepared Poison Dagger attacks

Reference: D2R Reign of the Warlock **3.3.93847**, with approved Sanctuary deviations taking precedence. This candidate follows the combined-poison fix at `9c5773b` and is staged in `.local/class-skills/poison-dagger-attack`. It is not installed.

`PoisonDaggerCombat` uses the shared `SourceMeleeResult` decision order. Its player adapter passes the skill percentage into the existing attack-rating pool, checks the mapped recipient shield after a successful hit roll, and captures successful/get-hit/block flags. Misses and blocked attacks do not roll poison or weapon damage. Missing shield mappings are explicitly refused before windup/payment rather than invented.

The start prepares weapon damage, poison and mitigation before the action event, then pays mana once. The stored immediate total includes the mitigated poison rate once, as in the reviewed older calculator; later poison ticks remain separate. This is an explicit older-source bridge whose current executable equivalence is unobserved. A pure-poison hit does not request weapon hit recovery. Release uses the captured packet and drain context; it does not reroll the hit, shield or poison, recalculate changed equipment/resistance, or require another mana payment. The shared poison mitigation helper is factored from the unchanged immediate delivery path. An internal optional allocated-poison field lets this prepared attack commit its captured result; existing weapon callers retain their original path.

The pinned row enables `AttackNoMana`, so insufficient mana starts a basic attack. Actual steps, explicit location changes, Stop, weapon removal, hit recovery, respec and history cleanup discard a pending Poison Dagger attack. This closes the away-and-return relocation hole without changing the installed native kernel. The pending record is transient across reload; a paid start does not replay or refund after loading.

### Shared mechanic evidence and limits

- Block recovery uses25fps source frames and effective FBR, including the Amazon one-handed swing animation speed. Class baselines, a breakpoint, pending-action cancellation and preserved cooldown are tested. Holy Shield and transformations await their class mechanics. Source: [D2MOO block animation calculation](https://github.com/ThePhrozenKeep/D2MOO/blob/0c0bbd1e6bef9181a3da1c046523af622a925416/source/D2Common/src/Units/Units.cpp), cross-checked against the [OpenDiablo2 animation fixture](https://github.com/OpenDiablo2/OpenDiablo2/blob/master/d2common/d2fileformats/d2animdata/testdata/AnimData.d2), SHA-256 `e3a54b9ab71df7ccdf3655bbba5868c9d53cd8716b0aad25e8c3d5621579cecb`. UO animation20 supplies the current recovery visual.
- Knockback is one native NPC movement step. It bypasses voluntary recovery only within that synchronous step and preserves facing/deadline; terrain, occupancy, doors, regions, frozen/paralyzed restrictions and movement events remain authoritative. Tests cover real Zombie recovery and a blocking wall. Player combat remains outside this PvE consumer.
- Elemental flat reduction precedes resistance; ordinary item pierce cannot break an immune monster and resistance bottoms at−100. Absorb applies percentage (capped40%) then flat, healing before hit-point damage. Actual native Raven Frost/Dwarf Star hits test hurt/full-health cases and absence of duplicate mitigation. Poison bypasses flat physical/magic reduction. Item cold pierce also affects chill length. Magic damage joins the actual strike packet; player magic resistance caps at75. XP gain applies after level penalties.
- Poison, Open Wounds and Prevent Heal suppress natural life regeneration through native player/creature hooks and the separate graveyard/pet recovery loops. Poison expiry resumes recovery without accumulated healing; mana regeneration and explicit healing are unchanged. Timed-effect persistence reads the previous version and stores the new fields.
- Native appearance exceptions affect only saved named gear, preserving ordinary race/gender restrictions. Every mapped eligible appearance is equipped and validated; a human-worn named Circlet survives actual saved-world boots. Native durability remains the project baseline with named flat durability additions; repair/fortification preserve identity and property rolls, and ethereal equipment cannot use those restoration paths.

- Independent item slow lasts30 seconds and uses source normal/unique/boss caps, with movement/attack floors. Cannot Be Frozen only suppresses cold. Target defence loss accumulates against the target and survives restart until death/reset. Freeze, blind and flee interrupt queued custom actions and use the actual native movement/target/AI gates, without owning native Frozen/Paralyzed flags. Blind/fear share an AI curse lane. Source: [SkillItem events8/9/14/19](https://github.com/ThePhrozenKeep/D2MOO/blob/0c0bbd1e6bef9181a3da1c046523af622a925416/source/D2Game/src/SKILLS/SkillItem.cpp), [damage/freeze handling](https://github.com/ThePhrozenKeep/D2MOO/blob/0c0bbd1e6bef9181a3da1c046523af622a925416/source/D2Game/src/UNIT/SUnitDmg.cpp), [blind duration](https://github.com/ThePhrozenKeep/D2MOO/blob/0c0bbd1e6bef9181a3da1c046523af622a925416/source/D2Game/src/SKILLS/SkillNec.cpp). The known inverted nonzero guard in the decompiled slow function is not reproduced.
- Damage-to-mana consumes actual incoming melee/ranged weapon loss once after resistance, absorb and Protection; poison, bleed and reflection are not weapon events. Running stamina reduction changes native running costs, preserving fractional debt through save/reload; walking, overweight and damage fatigue keep their independent rules. Recheck Energy Shield and new class damage delivery when those consumers are added.
- Self-repair and quantity replenishment use serial-only registries and immutable save snapshots, not world scans. The recurring interval is `max(125, 2500/rate + 1)`25fps frames (minimum5 seconds), following the pinned [item regeneration handler](https://github.com/ThePhrozenKeep/D2MOO/blob/0c0bbd1e6bef9181a3da1c046523af622a925416/source/D2Game/src/ITEMS/ItemMode.cpp). The initial interval uses that same bound. Self-repair works in storage, including ethereal gear, restores current durability only and does not revive a broken item. It does not enable ordinary ethereal repair or fortification. Throwing quantity is a saved counter on one native weapon, never Item.Amount: depletion cannot split/delete a named weapon; increased source capacity, misses, interruption, reuse after replenishment, cloning, storage, trade and real restarts are exercised.
- Non-ethereal throwing weapons can refill through the existing player/NPC repair service, including partial/full and combined work. Refill-only work preserves maximum/current durability, identity and quantity capacity; stale/changed-quantity requests, replay and insufficient payment are rejected. This follows the [official throwing-weapon repair path](https://classic.battle.net/diablo2exp/items/elite/throw.shtml). Sanctuary uses the already approved tier repair material/essence bundle and NPC fee per refill transaction, rather than wand essence per projectile; partial/full share that bundle. T48 must include this resource sink. Ethereal weapons remain excluded from paid repair/refill and can only replenish if they have the property.
- Source fire tags in all210 skill bindings feed generic learned/directly granted ranks without granting every unlearned fire spell. Magic enhancement/pierce and combined fire/lightning/cold ranges use the common elemental pipeline. Direct spells, procs, auras and charges still require their actual class effect/delivery implementation.
- Rest in Peace is a saved corpse flag, set on actual player kills and honored by player summons, monster revival and both native Animate Dead target/resolve stages. It does not carve/delete corpses or remove loot; new corpse skills must honor the same flag.
- Piercing allocates at most four continuations before impact, then traces the original ray within weapon range. Each target is attempted once, with native terrain/LOS, height and friendly checks; secondary hits pay no additional throwing quantity or weapon wear. Source: [missile creation](https://github.com/ThePhrozenKeep/D2MOO/blob/0c0bbd1e6bef9181a3da1c046523af622a925416/source/D2Game/src/MISSILES/Missiles.cpp) and [collision/continuation](https://github.com/ThePhrozenKeep/D2MOO/blob/0c0bbd1e6bef9181a3da1c046523af622a925416/source/D2Game/src/MISSILES/MissMode.cpp). This uses the existing class projectile release timing; new skill missiles must explicitly opt into the appropriate continuation rules.
- Cosmetic adaptations reuse native art: extra-blood creates a native blood decal on a damaging weapon hit, and complete golden sets pulse native gold particles. Natalya fade multiplies world body/equipment alpha through shared protocol0xF9/client patch0033; it changes neither Hidden nor body/hue/targeting. Partial, broken, unidentified or stored pieces suppress their complete-set visuals. Native saved-world tests cover removal/restoration and derived appearance ownership. No new bitmap assets were necessary.

Verification evidence is isolated under `.local/t49`: full cumulative and separate legacy four-boot suites, copied-live-save round trips,61 Python checks, the573-row/35-set/224-property standalone checker (692 set subsets), and exact replay of47 native patches across97 paths and33 client patches across41 paths. All four actual-client gambling/insurance/escrow/wardrobe suites pass, including a paid eligible Unique purchase and a one-draw unavailable Set refund; representative screenshots were inspected, including actual Natalya fade/removal/restoration, golden set particles and native blood. Paid throwing refills cover partial/full, player/NPC, stale quantities, replay, insufficient resources and ethereal refusal. No runtime or test assembly is installed into production by these checks.

# T11 — vendors, supplies and persistent sale quotes

All six defaults were confirmed on 20 October 2026 (JST). Implemented, tested and deployed to the local runtime; the server is running with the completed profession/crafting features enabled.

- Ordinary shops generate equipment at item level 41, using Normal bases. Britain generates at 70 and may also stock Exceptional bases. Existing equip requirements apply. All supported standard equipment identities participate; cosmetic clothing remains White. Exotic materials deferred in T08 remain excluded.
- Each equipment seller has nine individual White and three individual Magic offers. Unsold stock and its hourly refresh deadline persist. Reopening, changing customers and restarting do not reroll an unexpired offer. Purchasing transfers the exact displayed item and its saved properties.
- Drop/Crafted equipment receives its identified sale quote once, when first listed for sale: Normal 100–500, Exceptional 500–1,000, Elite 2,500–5,000. Existing saved quotes remain fixed. Unidentified pays one tenth without revealing hidden properties; starter gear pays one gold. These replace the prototype resale percentage/caps.
- Shop asking prices are twice the generated tier valuation for White and four times for Magic. Purchased gear retains Vendor origin and its original paid price; resale pays ten percent. Legacy Vendor items without that record pay one gold. Trading, equipping, copying and reloading preserve those records. Sold items are consumed and never become buyback or restock goods.
- Tools cost 10 gold, with the existing 20–30-use supply rule for the ten crafting tools. Reagents and bottles use existing native prices; ordinary food ingredients cost 1–5 gold and cloth costs 20 per unit. Supplies stay available. Gathering tools retain their native use mechanics. Existing functional Healing/Mana restoratives through Greater are supplied at their pinned D2 base prices; Super remains outside vendor supply.
- Suppliers and all ten crafting/three gathering trainers cover Britannian towns on the enabled Felucca and Trammel facets. Native shop/spawn locations guide placement; missing professions share a suitable town workshop. Public crafting facilities accompany those professions. Britain alone has the higher equipment stock cap. Trainers retain the existing first-choice confirmation, active-craft rules and one gold per +0.1 up to 30.0.

Settings, stock ratios, prices and town placements are authored data. No NPC metal/wood/leather/scrap supply is introduced. Usable socket gems and bark remain tied to their later acquisition cards; native decorative gems do not become socket items. Identification progression, repairs, gambling and broader recipe/effect catalogues retain their own cards.

## Authored data and persistence

`market-settings.json` holds stock levels, counts, the refresh period, asking-price multipliers, refund percentage and sale bands. `market-supplies.json` holds supply types/prices. The old percentage/cap resale settings were removed from `economy.json`; that file continues to configure salvage/Imbuing. Scissors and gathering tools retain their native charge-free processing/gathering mechanics. Each displayed crafting tool's saved 20–30-use capacity is also the capacity delivered on purchase.

`town-markets.json` declares 476 service sites: 17 towns on each of Felucca and Trammel, with fourteen roles per town (the thirteen skill trainers plus a jeweller). Ocllo is the Felucca counterpart of Trammel's New Haven. The catalogue covers Britain, Cove, Jhelom, Minoc, Trinsic, Vesper, Yew, Wind, Serpent's Hold, Skara Brae, Nujel'm, Moonglow, Magincia, Buccaneer's Den, Delucia and Papua as well. Inputs are the pinned native region/vendor-spawn data; `tools/reference/town-markets-evidence.json` records their hashes and the native revision. Reproduce with `python3 tools/build_town_markets.py --check`.

Saved merchant IDs prevent duplicate town populations. Public stations/forges are saved real items and re-registered on load. Placement finds usable floors near authored anchors, including dock/boat anchors whose original boat is absent. All 476 merchant floors, role skills and public workstation references are checked in the integration suite. Native merchants of corresponding professions receive the same stock rules.

`SanctuaryMarkets` version 0 stores each merchant's role/site, shelf, workstation/forge IDs, next refresh and individual offers. Real stock items live on a private saved shelf. Buy packets cannot lift stock directly, claim another merchant's offer, repeat an already purchased item or purchase player-sold buyback goods. Refreshes replace unsold equipment while retaining their hourly schedule; supplies remain available. Buying transfers the actual offered equipment rather than rolling a replacement. Ordinary purchases can use bank funds at the existing native threshold of 2,000 gold; staff retain native free-purchase privileges and those items record zero paid gold.

`SanctuaryEquipment` version 3 adds the original vendor purchase price; versions 0–2 retain their defaults and existing metadata. Saved sale offers and asking prices remain independent of future price-band tuning. Old Vendor items without a paid-price record sell for one gold; normal player purchases refund ten percent of their recorded price. No sale changes origin. Protected/quest/insured items and equipped goods cannot be sold, while designated newbie starter gear can sell for one gold. Existing non-equipment acceptance/prices are retained where native shop information provides them.

The complete exchange is validated before payment or delivery. Duplicate/foreign/stale requests, insufficient funds and capacity failures consume nothing. Sales delete sold inputs and cannot create buyback stock. The server supplies buy and sell property lists through the existing ClassicUO shop interface. Unidentified item properties remain hidden and the sale quote remains ten percent of its underlying saved offer.

## Verification workflow

Build native content without staging a running server, build `Sanctuary.Content` and the integration assembly, then run the disposable harness with `SANCTUARY_TEST_CREATION=1 SANCTUARY_TEST_PROFESSIONS=1 SANCTUARY_TEST_MARKETS=1`. The market suite checks every supported stock base/tier, all town floors/roles, actual training for all thirteen skills, fixed rolls/quotes, buying/bank payments, copied/traded vendor records, full packs and duplicate/stale requests. It changes price bands in its disposable data before restarting and confirms old quotes/stock remain fixed.

The separate actual-client harness uses `SANCTUARY_TEST_UI=1 SANCTUARY_TEST_COMBAT_UI=1 SANCTUARY_TEST_MARKET_UI=1`, with `SANCTUARY_TEST_UI_STATE` and `SANCTUARY_TEST_LOG` pointing to disposable evidence files. It buys the displayed Magic sword, hovers its stats, sells an unidentified sword at its ten-percent quote, checks hidden tooltips and verifies no buyback. Screenshots are retained locally as `t11-shop-*.png`. No client source change is required.

# Complete Consume owner-state and action rule

Prepared Sanctuary adaptation against **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. The [pinned suppliers](consume-suppliers-checkpoint.md) authenticate the common skill values and three original demons' `monpet` formulas. Current start69/do159 remains unreadable in the retained reference. This proposal defines the missing action, world, state-writer and pool contract; it does not claim to recover those callbacks. **The learned route remains gated pending this complete adaptation decision.**

1. **Supported demons and selection.** Require a living, connected Warlock/Witch with Consume trained and one of their original learned Goatman, Tainted or Defiler bodies. Require its original owner, saved creation order, valid controller and active roster membership. Outside a safe area it must be alive, unparked, on the same facet, outside town, within 19 native tiles and in the owner's LOS. Refuse unsupported actors, converted equipment contexts, dead/deleted bodies and other owners' pets. Bind Demon remains unsupported until its own host is qualified. Consume is right/quick-only, matching the pinned row; no left binding is invented. A roster menu selects the actual owned body, with its current bonus preview. In an actual safe area, allow that owner's parked roster through the same menu rather than requiring hidden bodies to be clicked or banning an `InTown=1` skill. Revalidate identity and eligibility at release; a stale menu cannot restore a retired body.
2. **Action and payment.** Use the existing SC/FCR cast clock, ordinary form/overlap/incapacitation guards and shared resource checks. Spend no mana during windup. Stop, movement including moving away and back, weapon changes and interruption cancel unfinished work. On release, capture current effective Consume rank, hard Death Mark points and configured facet difficulty; validate the payload and resources, then pay the table cost once. Rank 1 costs 25 mana; rank 20 costs 44. There is no extra local cooldown. A failed precommit costs nothing and retires no demon. Claim the pending action before callbacks so it cannot replay.
3. **Retirement and failure boundaries.** Remove the demon's live registration and delete its original body; this is retirement, not an attack or kill. It creates no corpse, loot, kill credit or death-triggered explosion, and parking/save reload cannot bring it back. Install the owner's captured buff and enforce the shared pool. If an unsupported late callback fails after commitment, retain the paid mana and completed retirement/state changes, log the refusal and do not invent rollback or replay.
4. **Common owner bonuses.** Add the captured maximum-life percentage to the existing skill-life pool, alongside Battle Orders and other bonuses. It is +5% at rank 1 and +24% at rank 20. Entering, replacing, expiring or evicting the state preserves the owner's current life percentage through the existing exact-resource transition; do not heal, refill or independently multiply other life bonuses. Add captured diminishing effective run/walk speed to the existing skill velocity pool, preserving armor/item terms and final clamps. Rank 20 contributes 40 effective points. No new movement or recovery timer is created.
5. **Typed bonuses.** Goatman contributes off-weapon enhanced damage of `Consume rank × difficulty factor + 20`, with factors 1/3/5 for Normal/Nightmare/Hell, and Crushing Blow of `5 + hard Death Mark`. Combine Crushing Blow with item chance in the existing single callback/roll, capped at 100%; do not create an independent extra proc. Tainted contributes fire damage mastery `min(rank+9,20)` and fire resistance piercing `min(rank+4,15)`. Defiler contributes magic mastery `min(rank+9,20)` and magic piercing `min(rank+4,10)`. Reuse the existing element/mastery/mitigation services, including immunity restrictions. These are owner bonuses, not new inheritance onto already-snapshotted pets or traps. Replacing the state removes its previous species' bonuses.
6. **Snapshot and lifetime.** Keep one Consume payload per owner; a successful recast replaces it, even with a lower rank, and starts a fresh lifetime. Capture ranks, hard synergy and species at release. Later equipment/training changes do not rewrite it. Rank 1 lasts 40 seconds and rank 20 lasts 420 seconds, on an exact UTC interval with an exclusive expiry. Logout, town parking and saves do not restart the clock or provide recovery. Owner death/deletion or loss of the Warlock profile removes it through the shared maintenance path. Expiry uses the existing 40 ms lifecycle tick. Treat its logical summon slot like the approved difficulty-rescaling summons: on a facet-difficulty change, reevaluate only difficulty-sensitive bonuses using captured ranks/species; preserve order, original body identity and deadline. This consumed-slot projection is an explicit adaptation.
7. **Shared summon pool.** The buff occupies one slot in the same source pool as the original demons. Give it the consumed body's original creation order, not a fresh order. Combine logical buff and actual bodies, enforcing the existing hard-Mastery pool limit with the same oldest-first policy. A newer summon may evict the buff if that slot is oldest, or retire an older live demon while retaining the buff. Buff expiry frees the slot. Reserve the retired slot's saved order after reload so new summons cannot reuse that order and reverse FIFO. Native creature serials may be reused after deletion; the saved creation order distinguishes the original retired body from a new creature with the same serial. Future Bind Demon must join this same pool when its actor exists; a distinct pet type is not a separate pool.
8. **Save compatibility.** Persist the validated owner payload, original body identity, creation order and exact timestamps in a named `SanctuaryConsume` profile, initial version 0 and load priority 250. Priority is an ordering value, not a persistence ID. Existing profile/body serialization versions remain unchanged. Old saves have no Consume state. Reject malformed/duplicate payloads atomically. The consumed body stays absent; existing surviving summons retain births, wounds, random streams and controllers. The owner can retain fractional current life above its unbuffed maximum through reload; no full-health reset or delayed refill is allowed.
9. **Assets and verification limits.** Use identified generic owner effect `0x375A` and sound `0x1E5` while final sacrifice/cast/owner overlay and Consume audio are missing. Talent-tree and right/quick-hotkey icons remain separately missing. Assets do not gate mechanics. Prepared/native tests cover this rule, actual stat/combat consumers, original saved bodies and the real roster menu/cast callbacks. They do not certify actual socket ability inputs, current protected callback equivalence, future bound monsters, all PvP/global-world/client behavior or full class completion. Those limits must remain explicit when the learned route is admitted.

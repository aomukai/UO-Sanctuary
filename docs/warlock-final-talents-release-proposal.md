# Four remaining Warlock talents: release decision

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**, approved Sanctuary deviations first. This consolidates a decision on the four prepared talents needed to reach30/30Warlock handlers. **It does not approve or enable them.** Their original frozen proposals and receipts remain unchanged. Current D2R callbacks could not be recovered, so adopting these rules would record explicit Sanctuary adaptations rather than claim modern executable parity.

| Talent | Proposed behavior that needs a decision | Full original rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Blood Oath | Redirect one total budget from settled direct damage after shields, split across up to three eligible demons in saved order. Effective rank1redirects3%, rank20redirects25%. Each share is capped by that demon's life; untransferred damage remains on the owner. No recursive sharing or new attack/leech event. | [Blood Oath](blood-oath-damage-proposal.md) |
| Engorge | Feed one eligible corpse to heal nearby demons and give captured armor, physical resistance, attack speed, recovery and percentage-based physical melee leech. A caster/target120mslease prevents simultaneous explosions from stacking on that enemy. | [Blood Boil](blood-boil-rule-proposal.md) |
| Consume | Permanently retire a selected owned demon for a captured timed owner buff. The buff occupies its original summon-pool slot/order; species changes replace bonuses. Preserve relative owner life, absolute expiry and the existing oldest-first pool. A safe-area roster menu can select parked demons. | [Consume](consume-world-rule-proposal.md) |

The release also includes the already-prepared extensions for **qualified bound demons**, which the older original-species proposals deliberately left for later work:

- [Shared recipients and mixed Blood Boil](bind-demon-recipients-checkpoint.md): the same original/bound roster, order, life threshold/cost, explosions and recipient delay; no extra cost formula or guessed actor mapping.
- [Bound Blood Oath](bind-demon-oath-checkpoint.md): the same total budget and remainder/life-cap rules. Admit only the qualified identity native-damage multiplier case; unsupported modifiers/overrides refuse protection rather than silently using other pets.
- [Bound Engorge](bind-demon-engorge-checkpoint.md): persist the captured payload on the original bound host and use the existing native armor/resistance/attack-rate/recovery and physical-melee leech services.
- [Bound Consume](bind-demon-consume-checkpoint.md): retain pinned typed numeric bonuses, learned-only skill bonuses, resistance/pierce/cap consumers and original retirement/pool order. The prepared maximum-magic-resistance projection uses base75, additive maximum and cap95. These native projections are adaptations, not recovered hidden getters.
- [Consume auras](consume-aura-checkpoint.md): four pinned aura-bearing recipes reuse fixed-rank Holy Fire, Meditation or Salvation providers, independent of the selected aura, with no mana payment or invented hard Paladin synergies. Existing startup, lease priority, expiry and offline-skip behavior retain.

Unmapped actors, unqualified native modifiers/equipment/Sunder and unsupported recipients remain explicit refusals. This does not make arbitrary UO creatures bindable, invent missing demon abilities or change the approved bodies: Gargoyle, Succubus and Gazer. Summon snapshots, safe-area parking without recovery/reset, difficulty rescaling and preserved life percentage remain in force. Consume remains right/quick-only; Blood Oath remains passive. Corpse targeting and the other skills' pinned bindings retain.

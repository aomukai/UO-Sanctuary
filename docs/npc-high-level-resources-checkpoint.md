Reference: D2R Reign of the Warlock **3.3.93847**. This correction follows learned Iron Golem acceptance at `6b18590`; the staged build is not installed.

The calculator now applies the level-99 cap only to player inputs. NPC levels remain positive and retain their source value; no summon-level clamp or player stat coefficient is added.

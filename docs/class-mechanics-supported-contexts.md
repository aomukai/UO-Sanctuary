# Class mechanics: supported contexts and deferred systems

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. Approved Sanctuary rules take precedence. This boundary review uses the current implementation and preserved class cohorts, rather than the obsolete pending descriptions retained in early checkpoints.

## Ordinary class play

All eight classes have 30 registered talents. Their existing class cohorts account for 210 learned active routes and 30 passive entries, with dedicated damage/stat/control/summon, resource, cancellation and saved-lifecycle consumers in the class coverage matrices. The closing Bash and Find Item changes have their own focused qualification and installed source bridge. Approved pet upgrades, caps, snapshots, safe-area parking, original identity, wound preservation and facet scaling retain their separate Necromancer/Druid/shared consumer evidence.

The user's intent-based authority resolves smaller unrecoverable native mapping details by reusing existing verified services. Existing UO terrain/LOS/safety, integer tile geometry, original actor ownership, ordered typed health, and explicit supported source/encounter profiles remain the shared world contract. The [closing package](class-closing-package-checkpoint.md) records the remaining Barbarian landing, cancellation and recipient decisions. This is a Sanctuary implementation contract, not a claim of identical unpublished D2R executable behavior.

## Iron Golem and Revive

Iron Golem uses its actual identified metal original item. `IronGolemCasting.Plan` validates the item, accessibility, placement, all three difficulty recipes and combat-event consumers before committing. Release revalidates; a failed candidate returns the exact original and preserves the old golem. Existing item identity, numerical equipment graph, ordinary typed combat, source poison, defenses, resources and admitted event/proc consumers retain their dedicated evidence. An unsupported original projection fails explicitly; it is never silently stripped or replaced with invented stats. Crafted/material/socket combinations and unsupported item events remain item-system admission work, not a missing Iron Golem handler.

Revive restores the original eligible NPC with its existing abilities. It does not impose a separate spell whitelist. Loot remains on the corpse while captured combat effects stay with the original monster. No expiration timer is added. `NativeReviveRules` uses a pinned eligible source or an explicit native-role mapping; `ReviveCasts.Plan` refuses missing pre-death eligibility, disabled proposals and unsupported ownership/equipment contexts. The approved native Lich is a concrete mapped case; Hollow One supplies only its eligibility analogue, not a replacement spell repertoire.

Unclassified native species must be assigned a role deliberately. Relative strength alone does not create boss/champion/unique flags. Future ordinary-monster strength/density or players-5 balancing must not change eligibility by accident. Global conversion of living UO caster monsters to D2 spells is the separately requested world task and will automatically apply to their revived versions.

## Clearly retained limitations

- Exact undisclosed modern RNG, frame/getter/callback, collision and multicaster equivalence remains uncertain wherever the pinned capture could not establish it. Per-skill references and explicit Sanctuary proposals retain that provenance; successful local checks establish the chosen implementation, not unknown modern behavior.
- Unsupported native PvP/special-recipient, elite/attachment or original-item contexts remain explicit refusals. The current ordinary PvE source/encounter catalogue is the tested recipient scope. Expanding the world catalogue needs matching classification and consumer checks.
- Telekinesis cannot activate absent D2-only portals, waypoints or shrines. New world services need their own interaction endpoints; inventing a class-specific placeholder service would not supply that system.
- Complete future charged gear, crafting/oskill designs and unadmitted item-property combinations remain separate item work. Existing qualified item effects continue to use the class handlers. Charms and runewords are not planned.
- Effects/audio and talent/hotkey icons have separate 240-entry registers with identified placeholders. Final artwork is later asset work; a missing final image does not gate a mechanical action.
- Global monster spell conversion, density/strength/XP/drop balance and later melee targeting/mana quality-of-life design remain separate smaller goals.

These boundaries do not authorize silently inventing a missing mechanic. Any newly discovered defect inside the qualified ordinary class-play contract still requires a fix and a focused regression check.

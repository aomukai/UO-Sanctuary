# Double Swing mechanics and approved startup

Reference: D2R Reign of the Warlock **3.3.93847**, with the user's explicit [startup/payment/timing approval](approved-double-swing-rule.md) taking precedence over unresolved current code. The exact prompt and answer are pinned in [the decision record](../tools/reference/double-swing-start-decision.json). The current compiled start68 and paired IAS/WSM quirks are not claimed as verified.

The learned route requires a supported native pair of melee weapons. It uses the shared sequence rate, adds the table's 50% skill speed in the same additive layer as the sequence penalty, and schedules the two current SQ11 events. The primary and secondary have separate `WeaponHands` scopes. Ordinary `PlayerWeaponContact` preparation/release supplies accuracy, block, weapon/element/poison damage, item events, durability, drains, native notifications and death processing. Double Swing supplies the pinned AR formula and hard-point-only Bash damage; it does not create a second damage pipeline. Melee use of eligible throwing weapons uses the existing melee context.

The approved rule spends table mana at each released strike: rank 1 costs 1 mana each, rank 2 costs 0.875 each, rank 8 costs 0.125 each and rank 9+ is free. An underfunded start becomes one ordinary primary-hand attack at ordinary attack timing. If mana becomes insufficient between the two events, that remaining contact uses ordinary accuracy/damage rather than the skill bonuses. Released misses still pay. Stop, movement (including moving away and back), facet changes, lost/broken hands, incapacitation, respec, disconnect/logout and death cancel remaining work without refunding a released strike. Mouse release ends repetition while the started cycle can finish. Reload does not replay cancelled work.

The second hand uses the shared GUID successor/wrap selector over the source melee-reach-plus-four-subtile search radius, mapped through the existing two-subtiles-per-tile convention. Candidates need not already be hittable: pinned weapon reach and the shared player/creature footprint/collision predicate decide contact separately at preparation and release. This also avoids inheriting a throwing weapon's projectile range. A lone original target can be selected again. Native geometry and recipient guards remain explicit; the old do70 source does not establish every modern room, special-recipient or target-ordering branch.

Current read-only executable inspection anchors all **322 classic descriptors** and all **14 SQ11 weapon profiles**. Each contains 17 pose rows, with action markers at indices 4 and 9. Pose modes are ATTACK1 (7) and SPECIAL3 (15); they are not inferred to be a generic ATTACK2 animation. Raw auxiliary 256 is retained without assigning it an unverified role. Five complete older scheduler/reader/player-callback/frame bodies execute **135 supplied speed/entry/fraction contexts** under ASan/UBSan. Their event ordinals are 0/1 and preserve FP8 low bits. The native scheduler compares every context against every profile (**1,890 comparisons**) and retains existing SQ21/24/25 loading. At supplied advance 256, actions occur at entry+4/+9 and end at +17; that supplied-rate fact is distinct from the approved real attack-rate choice. Legacy do70/GUID proof retains its separate 7,536 controls.

Native acceptance covers normal and throwing melee pairs, independent paid hands and notifications, source GUID wrap, fractional/zero costs, early/replay refusal, ordinary low-mana fallback, misses, mid-series funding changes, eleven cancellation cases, original pair/gear/durability/wound/resource preservation and real left/right/quick packets.

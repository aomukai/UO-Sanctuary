# T28 — Restorative mixtures and potion supply

Use a mortar and pestle, choose **Craft restorative mixtures**, select a recipe and review its ingredients/chance before brewing at a public Alchemy station. The active profession must be Alchemy. Each success produces one fixed-effect potion; quality does not increase its power or output count.

| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Instant Heal | 50 | 3 Super Healing | Fill life |
| Instant Recharge | 60 | 3 Super Mana | Fill mana |
| Rejuvenation | 70 | 3 Greater Healing + 3 Greater Mana | Restore35% of maximum life and mana |
| Full Rejuvenation | 80 | 3 Super Healing + 3 Super Mana | Fill both pools |

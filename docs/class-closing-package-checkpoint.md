## Remaining Barbarian edge rules

Under the user's delegated intent-based adaptation authority, Sanctuary keeps the existing safe native behavior rather than claiming inaccessible modern callbacks were recovered:

- A zero-distance Leap Attack request fails before allocation or payment. Adjacent enemy leaps still use the separately qualified modern-style flight.
- Flight captures its destination. If that tile becomes occupied, abort at a legal landing using the existing safe-landing helper, preferring the destination when legal and otherwise the origin/fallback. Failed landings and lost reservations emit no area or weapon strike; an already-paid flight is not refunded.
- Moving the initial enemy does not move the landing destination. Normal post-landing reach and target rules still govern the later weapon contact.
- A broken primary or secondary hand invalidates Double Throw's pair. Before entry, no mana/ammunition is spent; after the first release, the remaining event cancels without extra payment or ammunition use and without refunding the first release.
- Existing dedicated Leap Attack evidence already verifies deferred lethal damage: death completes at landing and emits no area or weapon damage. It was not rerun merely to repeat that proof.

Its prior dedicated checks include 390 damage vectors, 160 monster-block vectors, actual champion/unique recipients and two reloads.

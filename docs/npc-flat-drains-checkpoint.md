`NpcFlatDrains` implements the complete older non-hireling/non-transformed monster drain branch: after attacker/defender damage events and physical clipping, capped life/mana/stamina drain can restore the NPC’s missing life using the victim’s drain effectiveness. Victim HP is committed next, then mana and stamina are deducted, with the source’s below-one-point cutoff only for positive drain. Status delivery and death events follow. Recovery belongs to the NPC, never its player owner. There is no player difficulty percentage conversion in this branch.

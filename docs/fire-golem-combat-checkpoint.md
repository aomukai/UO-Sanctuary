# Fire Golem combined combat checkpoint

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**; approved Sanctuary deviations take precedence. Fire Golem and Necromancer remain unfinished.

New learned Fire Golems now share the existing unarmed NPC damage, elemental, intrinsic-critical and saved random services. Their captured skill fire joins their physical attack in one packet. This replaces the historical subtype's separate follow-up fire hit. Owner equipment, poison, critical and later skill changes are excluded; actual Enchant on the pet accrues into its fire range. Historical plain NecromancerMinion saves retain their existing type and path.

At captured rank 4 in Normal, the supported components have physical endpoints **2,560–6,911 FP256** and skill-fire endpoints **9,472–14,591 FP256**. Each upper endpoint is exclusive: 27 and 57 whole points respectively. A 5% intrinsic critical doubles both channels once. These are component ranges, **not the complete Fire Golem tooltip damage**: intrinsic Holy Fire's weapon contribution and the optional monster fire component remain separate unfinished work. Existing external aura leases still use the ordinary shared consumer.

Mapped NPC recipients now use the shared prepared contact path. Hit/block and mitigated physical/fire damage are captured at attack start, then the stored packet is released through the shared health/return services. Changes to resistance after allocation do not reroll it. Shields prevent both components before damage fill; range failure, cancellation and duplicate release cannot repeat damage. Life Tap and percentage Thorns use the physical component, with healing before returned damage. Unsupported recipient capabilities report the existing native bridge before allocation; later allocation faults do not retry through that bridge.

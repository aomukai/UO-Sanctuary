Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**, with approved Sanctuary decisions first and pinned older source as supplementary evidence. Goal active; 185 handlers / Necromancer 29, no class complete. Learned Iron Golem remains disabled. Candidate remains isolated from the installed build and saves.

`NpcEquipmentDamage.Roll` now produces an immutable offensive snapshot with actor/target identities, physical/elemental/poison rolls, critical results, cold duration and poison credit owner. It performs no HP, poison-state or delivery events. The existing live original-item strike consumes this shared roll through its existing mitigation/poison/event path. This separates calculations needed by the future ordered consumer without changing the legacy delivery order. The snapshot is **not** a fully mitigated/allocated source combat packet.

`PreparedMonsterCredit` registers the native whole display-HP loss before the ordered source HP write. It uses native `RegisterDamage`, including the controlled creature's real damage-master attribution. Fractional hits that cross no native HP boundary register zero; lethal credit caps to the equivalent native displayed life loss. It does not call `Damage`/`OnDamage` or enter death early. Reentrant credit callbacks that mutate life or remove the recipient refuse explicitly. Native damage notifications and full actor mode/event adapters remain separate work.

The new native checks compare 64 ordinary `SkillHealth.Damage` cases against ordered prepared releases using an actual owned Iron Golem: fractional starting life, tiny and whole hits, absorb healing and overkill produce identical HP, native credit and Responsible-owner amounts. Twelve snapshot controls verify no roll-time HP/states, preserved identities/owner, immutability after item deactivation and identical source RNG continuation when live delivery runs. A real Iron Golem prepared-policy kill awards its living owner XP exactly once and consumes corpse reward entitlement. This does not certify owner credit when the attacker has already been removed.

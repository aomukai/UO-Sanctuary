# T24 — Mining gems and upgrades

Successful ore extraction makes one independent 5% gem roll. Trained Base Mining unlocks Chipped/Flawed/Standard/Flawless/Perfect at 40/50/60/70/80. Skill bonuses do not unlock qualities. Failed extraction, sand, granite and other harvesting do not roll. A successful find announces its name and goes into the backpack, or at the character's feet with a message if it cannot fit. Failed delivery of the primary ore does not suppress the gem roll.

The authored starting distribution is one gem, uniform among all nine families and independently uniform among unlocked qualities. These distribution, quantity and delivery defaults were proposed during T24 and adopted after the optional tuning questions remained unanswered; they are configurable in `data/Sanctuary/Balance/socket-gems.json`.

The family mapping was confirmed on 20 October 2026. Amethyst, Diamond, Emerald, Ruby and Sapphire retain their Diablo II counterparts; Citrine uses Topaz and Tourmaline uses Skull. Their five-quality weapon/shield/armour values follow [Blizzard's gem tables](https://classic.battle.net/diablo2exp/items/gems.shtml). Amber adds flat Attacker Takes Damage 4/8/12/16/20. The user selected Star Sapphire's flat Magic Damage Reduced 1/2/3/4/5. Both added families use the same values in every supported slot. The checked-in catalogue records every numeric effect, elemental range and duration; `tools/build_socket_gems.py` reproduces it.

Each new `SocketGem` freezes its family, quality and effect payload. Copy, split, stack, transfer and save/reload retain that identity. Stacks require identical family, quality, frozen effects and origin. Tooltips label the effects as recorded for later insertion: **gem installation and actual effect activation are T26**. T24 supplies all 45 collectible identities without activating tooltip-only gear bonuses.

At a public Tinkering bench, use **Upgrade socket gems** on tinker's tools, target an owned gem, review and confirm. The authored starting recipe is three gems of the same family and quality for one of the next quality, with trained Tinkering 40/50/60/70 for Flawed/Standard/Flawless/Perfect output. Eligible upgrades are guaranteed, use one tool charge with crafted-tool saving, and offer ordinary native gain checks from the requirement through +50. There is no gold cost. Perfect gems cannot upgrade. These recipe defaults are configurable; they were adopted with the distribution defaults, rather than recorded as explicit user answers.

The native crafting timer and active-craft/station rules apply. Nested matching stacks can fund a recipe. Ownership, connection, profession, skill, tool and exact inputs are checked again at completion. Cancellation, stale/replayed requests and invalid capacity spend nothing. Delivery accounts for consumed inputs and compatible output stacks, so a full pack can still perform a valid exchange. No downgrade, bark upgrade or legacy-gem conversion exists.

Vendors buy usable gems for 25/75/250/500/1,500 gold by quality and never offer them back as stock. Current monster rewards and shops do not generate usable socket gems. Existing native gems retain their serials, amounts, art and names but are decoration-only: a tooltip states this, ordinary recipes cannot consume them, and they cannot fund upgrades or become socket gems. Native legacy rewards remain decorative. The future treasure-chest exception in L08 is still unresolved.

Verification covers all 45 saved identities and 36 upgrades, every chance ticket, Base-skill boundaries, family/quality selection, typed harvest exclusions, copy/split/stack/transfer/reload, prices, no resale stock, exact tool costs/saving, capacity and stale-input guards. The actual client exercises successful native ore extraction, a named gem find, the tool button, target, cancel, confirmation and native upgrade timer. Only the disposable fixture seeds extraction success and the rare reward ticket; production has no test setting.

The cumulative source/installed checks, separate legacy migration, 26 Python checks, patch reproduction and copied-world preservation are recorded with T25's deployment. Native changes are content-only: the typed successful-harvest callback and decorative gem tooltips. Engine, application, client and world populations are unchanged.

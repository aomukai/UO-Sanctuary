1. **Action and payment.** Require a living Warlock/Witch with effective Echoing rank and an eligible original melee weapon. Use the shared SC/FCR clock. Resolve the initial enemy/ground click once and lock that point during windup; reject a self-point or invalid/out-of-range/safe-area aim. Movement,facet or weapon changes and normal interruptions cancel remaining windup. Require the current fractional table cost at entry and release,spending it once only when all echoes can be created. Underfunded casts do nothing;there is no ranged basic-attack fallback. Empty lanes still pay and emit. Pending windups are transient and never resume from a save.
2. **Count and capture.** Capture the existing immutable source descriptor at release,including effective Echoing rank,hard Mirrored/Blade Warp,original selected weapon,weapon/item/Hex damage,off-weapon ED,species bonuses,poison metadata,critical chances,slow payload and Prevent Monster Heal. One plus hard Mirrored divided by5 gives1–5echoes;soft ranks do not add echoes. Later equipment or training changes do not rewrite released packets. The caster retains damage/kill credit;echoes are data-driven missiles,not summoned NPCs or extra follower slots.
3. **Physical composition.** Remove only the separately captured flat `normal-damage`/Damage+ledger from generic weapon endpoints;ordinary min/max affixes remain. Multiply those weapon endpoints by source116/128,then add the pinned skill-flat physical endpoints,which already include their hard synergy. At contact,roll that base,apply the shared additive owner/species ED and captured critical,then multiply physical by `(100 + calc1) / 100`. Thus the skill's own ED multiplies the whole physical result;its synergy appears in both its supplied flat endpoints and supplied ED. Signed FP256 overflow contexts refuse before payment rather than wrap.
4. **Elements and poison.** Multiply captured fire,cold,lightning,magic and poison-rate endpoints by116/128. Their existing captured mastery is not applied twice. Apply the echo's `calc6` and captured slow damage percentage to rolled elemental damage and poison rate;the physical-only skill ED and physical critical do not increase these channels. Preserve poison duration/source count,cold duration and life/mana-leech percentages. The actual two-source fixture retains175summed frames/count2/87impact frames while damage rate scales. Reuse ordinary weapon poison mitigation/replacement,cold and typed damage services;do not convert poison into an immediate damage lump or add spell-only Magical Penetration packets.
5. **Critical and RNG.** One captured critical decision per created echo,shared by its outward/return contacts. Use the existing unsigned passive→deadly→selected-weapon-mastery ordering,with the shared unconditional first passive draw. Double physical once. One cast source-compatible stream supplies these decisions and independent saved per-echo seeds;contacts advance their own damage stream. This is a scoped native RNG adapter,not a claim of cross-action D2R seed interleaving parity.
6. **Fan and speed.** A single echo follows the locked aim direction. Multiple echoes reuse the existing Teeth/Multishot source fan with their captured count,not a separately invented angle table. 7. **Outward and return flight.** Aim controls direction,not an early turn at the clicked monster. Travel outward for at most20updates,turning earlier at an actual wall/missile barrier from the prior clear position. Return toward the caster's current same-facet position,with remaining travel distance capped by that echo's actual outward distance. Moving farther away cannot extend its return. Catching the caster,exhausting the return budget or hitting a return-path barrier removes it. At most40mechanical updates are permitted;absolute expiry is at1640ms,so the40th update at1600ms remains possible and expiry wins before another update. Late ticks may process retained online steps,but a tick after absolute expiry removes the flight without replaying old hits.

Preflight a step's supported ordinary native recipients,then process each once in serial order for that update. A distinct **20-frame/800ms recipient lease per echo** implements the pinned NextDelay with separate cast identity;different echoes and casts are independent. The lease begins on qualified contact,including a blocked,avoided or immune hit;the exact expiry is eligible again. This deliberately chooses an explicit table-based rule despite older reports of no effective delay. It does not use a cast-wide lease or a one-hit-per-leg shortcut.

**Cold Mastery correction:** the receiver previously subtracted its full skill reduction once a monster's immunity had been broken. [Blizzard's patch2.6 notes](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23899624/diablo-ii-resurrected-ladder-season-three-has-concluded) specify one-fifth effectiveness after a break; the [pinned-build mechanics reference](https://diabloforfans.com/mechanics) corroborates the rule for supported immunity-breaking skills. The shared receiver now retains the resistance pool before Lower Resist/Conviction, excludes a known legacy elite Conviction offset, and divides only skill Cold Mastery by five for a broken ordinary-monster immunity. Unbroken immunity stays immune. Item pierce remains separate; positive cold-skill enhancement remains a damage modifier rather than resistance reduction. Sunder state integration and unmapped external/native modifier histories remain open.

Passives remain unavailable as independent active casts or hotkey actions.

Cold Mastery remains a live receiver reduction: changing hard mastery during an Ice Bolt flight changes effective resistance rather than the captured raw cold packet.

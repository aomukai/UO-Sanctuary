# Approved Frenzy mechanics

Mechanical reference: D2R Reign of the Warlock **3.3.93847**, with the [explicit user-approved startup/payment/charge rule](frenzy-startup-proposal.md) taking precedence. The exact answer is retained in [the decision record](../tools/reference/frenzy-start-decision.json). Current start67 and exact modern charge chronology remain unverified; the older release oracle does not certify them.

The learned route is implemented through `BarbarianPairedMelee`, shared with Double Swing. Both use the pinned current SQ11 data, scoped primary/secondary hands, shared source melee geometry, GUID successor/wrap targeting, ordinary weapon-contact preparation/release and cancellation. Double Swing keeps its separate 50% skill-speed addition. Frenzy uses the shared sequence penalty and its speed-charge bonus. Event deadlines are planned when a sequence starts; newly earned speed feeds subsequent sequence plans while immediately reaching the shared movement and attack-rate consumers. The existing sequence is not silently rebuilt mid-contact.

Each powered released Frenzy strike costs 1.5 mana, including a miss or block. An underfunded start makes one ordinary primary-hand attack; insufficient mana during the pair makes that remaining contact ordinary, without skill damage/AR/conversion or new charges. Shared item effects and ordinary attack mechanics still apply. Cancelled unreleased contacts stop without refunds.

Successful unblocked committed contacts grant a charge even when absorption prevents life loss. The total is capped at effective Frenzy rank when gained; that same event refreshes `150 + 10 × hard Increased Stamina` frames. The pinned movement/attack-rate curves are evaluated at charge count. Existing charges survive stopping, movement and weapon changes. Re-evaluation on the next gain applies the current rank cap, following the prepared stat-list update contract; exact modern gear-change chronology is not claimed. Death or invalid owner/skill removes eligibility. UTC expiration keeps running offline, with an immutable saved state and read-only worker serialization.

Damage, accuracy, critical, elemental/poison lanes, mitigation, events, durability and drains stay in the ordinary weapon services. Frenzy contributes its pinned AR and hard-only Double Swing/Taunt damage synergies; hard Berserk supplies physical-to-magic conversion through the already used conversion helper. A blocked, missed, out-of-range or refused contact grants no charge. Shared pair and recipient refusals remain explicit.

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. Explicit prepared releases now emit Fire Bolt, Charged Bolt, Nova and Frost Nova from an original Valkyrie. They reuse `SorceressSpellMissiles` movement, charged paths, radial acceleration, collision cells and per-cast hit-delay leases. Chain Lightning and original item-proc dispatch remain unfinished; learned Valkyrie is still disabled.

The optional saved `NpcItemSpellSource` retains the actual actor, original equipment binding and immutable numeric packet. Historical player flight JSON omits this field. The player owner supplies combat credit and hostility, while origin, damage, pierce, slow state and intrinsic critical belong to the NPC. Owner spell synergies, potions and equipment do not enter the packet. Source `monstats.valkyrie.Crit` is 5; the pet's persisted stream supplies that intrinsic roll independently from each child's damage seed. Item Critical Strike and weapon damage are not donated to these spells.

Child seed allocation remains a mandatory caller service. The prepared route does not guess original proc/factory RNG chronology or silently borrow the player/Mage random initializer. Entire volleys validate before one immutable pool update; invalid final children cannot leave partial volleys. Charged paths are copied. This API charges no player or pet mana and plays no player casting animation; the actual synchronous item execution frame and callback phase must still be bound before proc dispatch is enabled.

The shared `NpcElementSpellHit` generalizes the existing Hydra/Tainted post-defense fire receiver to fire, lightning and cold. It keeps ordered native notification, mitigation/absorb, HP, return, death/credit and packet retirement. `NpcFireSpellHit` delegates to that helper. Cold status commits through the actual emitter; player callers still use their player attacker. Captured pierce is explicit. This route admits living hostile NPC recipients with qualified native incoming/return services and zero source shield rate. Unknown/nonzero shield recipients and players refuse before the new damage/critical draws until their spell-defense phase is independently verified.

Offline, parked, off-facet or removed NPC sources do not fight; flight lifetimes continue without replay. A loaded flight ages elapsed inspection time harmlessly before movement resumes. This follows the existing NPC projectile host policy; exact current D2R source-removal behavior remains uncertain. Ring hit delays reuse the existing per-cast four-frame lease. Native room/cell enumeration, terrain and footprint projection retain their existing host adapters. The hit adapter composes existing return-fire, avoidance and intrinsic helpers; exact modern ordering of those phases still needs independent source acceptance.

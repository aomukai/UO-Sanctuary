## Lifecycle, saves and presentation

Use a saved node graph and per-cast recipient leases. Nodes retain original IDs, owner, map, position/velocity, remaining updates, rank/shape/packet, seed and UTC clocks. Restore validates the entire snapshot before replacing state. Validation bounds numeric/shape fields and reconstructs Bolt birth suppliers; cloud endpoint domains are validated rather than cryptographically proving their original derivation. Transient casting actions are not restored.

An owner who dies, is deleted or ceases to be a Warlock loses the graph. A released bolt/maker stops on logout, facet change or entering a safe area. Existing clouds stay where cast, do no damage while the owner is inactive, elsewhere or safe, and keep their original expiry; eligible return can resume their remaining lifetime. Parking never heals or resets them. Late clouds scan present occupants once rather than backfilling missed damage. Active bolt/maker frames can catch up against the current world; this is not a reconstruction of historical target positions. Already-expired clouds are removed before scanning.

A new scoped persistence profile stores the graph; it does not alter installed saves or existing profile versions. The prepared candidate has no automatic clock or learned binding. After approval it will join the existing projectile clock and receive actual left/right/quick input verification before being counted as implemented.

All art/audio remains explicitly temporary: native sound0x1E5, bolt0x36E4, cloud0x374A and contact0x375A with purple hue0x48D. Final cast/flight/maker/cloud/contact/expiry effects and sound belong in the effects register; talent and hotkey icons belong separately in the icon register. No final assets are generated here.

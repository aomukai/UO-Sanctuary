Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. This step supplies real numeric spell packets for the five proc spells found on original Valkyrie equipment: Fire Bolt, Charged Bolt, Frost Nova, Nova and Chain Lightning. It does not enable their item callbacks or learned Valkyrie casting.

It reuses `SkillValues` and the shared `DruidFirestorm.Endpoints` arithmetic. Owner spell ranks, hard synergies, equipment and potions are absent from this context. Item +skills do not increase the explicit proc rank. Unmaterialized skill-granting items still refuse through the existing original skill-list preflight.

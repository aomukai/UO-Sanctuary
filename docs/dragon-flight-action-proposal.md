# Dragon Flight action proposal

Status: **approved 2026-11-08 JST**; see [the recorded approval](dragon-flight-approved-action-rule.md). Original proposal wording is retained in the table for traceability. Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. Dragon Flight has its own unresolved payment and charged-contact boundaries; earlier Tail, Talon and Claw approvals are not inferred to cover it.

The prepared clock and world landing already have source/native coverage: SQ21 at fixed advance 256 emits teleport at frame 9, kick at frame 14 and completion at frame 23. The current owned attack-rate flag is disabled; IAS, FCR, WSM and slows do not alter this supplied ordinary-human clock. The existing landing adapter uses the current target position, source free-cell search and shared travel/occupancy/committed-transfer services. See [clock](dragon-flight-mode-checkpoint.md) and [landing](dragon-flight-landing-checkpoint.md).

| Boundary | Proposed Sanctuary rule | Basis or gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry | Require an ordinary trained Assassin, a living hostile NPC on the same map and the pinned inclusive squared 38-source-unit start range. Keep that initial target. | Current start12/table and shared world adapter. |
| Clock | Capture the fixed human SQ21 clock: teleport 9, kick 14, completion 23. | Prepared current data plus complete older mode/scheduler controls; exact modern executable parity remains unverified. |
| Resources | Require fifteen mana to begin, with no entry debit. At the teleport event, revalidate mana and landing. Spend fifteen once when committing the validated landing, before arrival hazards. Failed landing or a late mana shortfall cancels the entire action without payment or kick. | Explicit payment/failure adaptation. The older do52 ignores the warp helper's return and can still reach its kick after a failed warp; that behavior is not silently ported. |
| Fallback | If underfunded at entry, use the existing ordinary attack fallback. | Approved general melee preference and current `AttackNoMana` field. Ordinary attack still requires its own reachable target. |
| Landing | Use the target's current position at frame 9 without repeating the initial range check; reuse existing free-cell search, ground, travel, occupancy and transfer. The action's own transfer must preserve the pending kick. | Existing prepared source/native landing adapter. Native half-cell/room/footprint projection remains explicitly provisional. |
| Kick | At frame 14, check the original target again and allocate one current body/boot/typed/poison kick packet using current rank, gear and charges. Damage ED is `100 + 35 × (rank − 1)`; AR is `60 + 25 × (rank − 1)` plus existing charge AR. Ordinary weapon physical damage/critical do not replace the kick. | Current table and reusable prepared kick/contact services. |
| Charged contact | Charges bypass accuracy only; shield block/dodge remain. Each successful unblocked kick releases and consumes one charge per active family. Misses/blocks preserve charges; fully absorbed successful contact counts. | Separately proposed consume-one/hit adaptation. Exact modern charged defense and ordering remain unverified. |
| After landing | Misses, blocks, target loss or interruption after a committed landing do not refund its mana. If the target is no longer reachable, skip the kick rather than find a replacement. | Explicit target-loss/payment adaptation. |
| Cancellation | Stop, external movement/map changes, selected equipment changes, hit recovery, invalid owner state, death/respec/disconnect stop future stages. Claim stages before callbacks; no replay/refund. The paid/unpaid transient action never resumes after restart. | Existing shared action lifecycle. Arrival hazards keep their existing effects and may cancel the pending kick. |
| Target assistance | Direct selected-target casting is included. Ground-click leeway remains part of the separately recorded targeting pass until the current `SearchEnemyNear` wrapper is established or adapted. | Current table flags do not prove its hidden acquisition radius/order. |

Approval authorizes this action policy, not a claim of exact modern executable behavior. Before accepting the learned route, verify both real stages, correct payment/failure paths, kick/contact/charges, cancellation including the internal transfer, actual left/right/quick input, original equipment/resources and two real reloads. Retain existing Sorceress Teleport, Leap and dragon finisher checks. Full item/proc/owner/PvP, original geometry/event/RNG and rendered-client acceptance remain separate. Final departure/arrival, kick/contact, progressive-release effects/audio and talent/left/right/quick icons remain separately pending placeholders.

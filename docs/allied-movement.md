# Allied movement and occupied tiles

Class characters and their friendly pets can walk through one another without a stamina shove. Enemies, neutral bodies, walls, doors and unsupported floor remain obstacles. A planned stopping point must be clear; an occupied destination resolves to the closest reachable free tile. Allies can be intermediate steps along that route.

Each occupied tile has a holder. The unit already there keeps its normal attacks, spells, item use and AI. Only a later arrival sharing its body space becomes movement-only until it walks clear. A newcomer never causes the holder to yield, retreat or regroup. If the holder leaves voluntarily, the earliest remaining arrival becomes the holder. This also applies when a player walks onto a pet. Both occupants remain vulnerable to damage and area effects.

If an enemy blocks a passing pet's forward route, that pet seeks a free tile through the friendly line, retaining its retreat until clear. The allied holders stay in place and active, so retreat does not cascade backwards. The pet uses its normal movement speed and native terrain checks. After five continuous seconds without changing position while still passing through an ally, it regroups at a clear supported tile beside its summoner. Its identity, owner, current life and captured stats are retained. When no safe nearby tile exists it waits and retries. Players always walk clear themselves; they are never automatically relocated by this rule.

Movement-only is server enforced for attacks, spells, item use, native skills, equipment and inventory transfers, crafting, trade, loot and progression actions. Pending actions revalidate before spending resources; entering as a passer also cancels open secure trades. Damage, passive effects and elapsed timers continue. Client pathing receives the authoritative passing state and friendly serials; it cannot select an occupied endpoint or make an incumbent backtrack merely because another ally arrived. Ghosts hold no tile claim; a resurrected pet joins as a new arrival. Tile priority is transient and reconstructed deterministically for saved occupants after a restart.

Town Portal remains owner-only. An allied bystander can stand over a gate endpoint without blocking its creation or being transported. The owner lands on a clear supported tile within two tiles of the endpoint, with native travel and housing restrictions rechecked. If none is available, travel fails. Followers disperse beside the owner when clear space is available. Ordinary Teleport still requires a clear landing tile.

Integration checks cover low-stamina transit, incoming-player and incoming-pet action restrictions, retained holder actions, priority promotion and resurrection, damage, a blocked-front retreat through multiple allies without a chain reaction, safe five-second regroup, enemies, floors and occupied portal arrivals. Actual client checks cross a pet, reject belt use by the passing player, permit it for the incumbent player, and observe the passing pet regroup. Matching server and client builds use combat protocol 7 and belt protocol 3. Native changes are recorded in ModernUO patch 0035 and ClassicUO patch 0029; the engine itself is unchanged.

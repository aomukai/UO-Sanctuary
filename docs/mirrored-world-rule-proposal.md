# Mirrored Blades: complete proposed action/world rule

**Pending approval.** Reference **D2R Reign of the Warlock3.3.93847**;approved Sanctuary deviations first. Skill392and SQ25are pinned;current start68/do166and exact native emission/event chronology remain unavailable. The [prepared packet component](mirrored-packets-checkpoint.md) is retained. This defines the whole candidate rather than assuming those callbacks from Echoing Strike. Coverage remains229/240handlers,Warlock20/30;no class certified.

1. **Eligibility and aiming.** Require a living,online Warlock/Witch with effective Mirrored rank and an active original mapped non-potion weapon,outside actual safe areas. Support melee,bows,crossbows and throwing weapons together. Native players/class-pet/special/unmapped recipients retain the existing shared consumers' restrictions and diagnostics;no new D2 identity is invented. A selected melee enemy must initially be within shared weapon reach. Retain that enemy for the entire melee series;stop later copies when it dies or leaves reach. Ground melee chooses the nearest eligible enemy to the locked click within two tiles at release,also requiring actual weapon reach. Ranged selected enemies supply their current position at release if still valid;otherwise retain the clicked point. All missiles take that one direction,without a fan or homing. Native facing/weapon poses are placeholders for D2's floating weapon rig.
2. **Clock and interruption.** Use the owned weapon-specific SQ25 profile via WeaponAnimationClasses,including BOW/XBW and the source weapon class for throws. Use shared IAS,weapon speed,attack slow and skill attack-rate services plus the pinned calc3speed bonus;zero frame bonus. A single SQattack event releases primary then copies in ordinal order;do not invent one animation event per mirror. Underfunded entry uses the ordinary basic-attack clock. Movement,facet/weapon changes,freeze/paralysis,spells,hit recovery or normal Stop cancel remaining windup/sequence. Released projectiles remain independent. Pending actions are transient and never resume from saves.
3. **Capture and count.** At successful release,capture current original weapon,effective skill,off-weapon/species damage,item/Hex elements,poison metadata,leech,physical supplements and critical chances once. Later item/training changes do not rewrite emitted damage packets. Timing stays captured at entry. Total attacks are min(2+effective rank/5,5),so rank15reaches one primary and four copies. Soft ranks count. Skill damage/flat/element-conversion suppliers are zero;the absent Param8does not create a synergy. Every attack has its own accuracy and critical outcome. Use the existing unsigned passive→deadly→selected-weapon-mastery critical helper,one physical doubling,with one source stream per action and saved child streams per projectile. Exact cross-action D2R RNG interleaving remains unverified.
4. **Damage.** Source128preserves Damage+and ordinary min/max affixes. Primary damage is100%;copies use their supplied calc6percentage. Roll shared weapon/owner/species ED and physical critical first,then truncate the copy percentage independently on physical,fire,cold,lightning,magic and poison rate. Preserve poison length/source count,cold length and life/mana drain. Reuse shared physical supplements,mastery preparation,typed mitigation,cold and poison replacement;no spell-only Magical Penetration additions. Unlike Echoing,there is no source116reduction,Damage+subtraction,automatic accuracy or return flight. Unsupported numeric/supplier contexts refuse rather than wrap.
5. **Contacts and weapon effects.** Melee uses the shared ordered PlayerWeaponContact result/packet/native-reaction pipeline with captured allocation. Projectiles use ordinary accuracy and shared native shield block. Each qualified primary/copy may apply supported Crushing Blow,Open Wounds,item control,weapon Hexes,Life Tap,leech,incoming reactions and kill effects through existing services. Bane/Siphon retain their ordinary melee-attempt versus landed-projectile boundaries;Purge uses the shared landed/direct-damage phases. Damage is captured;live item/Hex/pierce eligibility remains the existing weapon consumers' rule. Ordinary chance-to-cast player items that the item system still marks unavailable are **not enabled or fabricated** here;their global registration/executor acceptance remains a separate property gap. This proposal does not certify every possible item effect.
6. **Crushing Blow and wear.** The primary uses ordinary shared divisors. A copy uses pinned calc7for ordinary melee NPCs,calc8for ranged NPCs,and calc9for controlled/player classification;double the selected NPC divisor for the shared elite/boss grouping. Ranged calc8already includes ranged reduction,so do not double it again. Existing HP/resistance/chance/credit rules remain authoritative. Unsupported PvP/class-pet contexts remain unqualified even though the numeric supplier is retained. Only the real melee primary can invoke normal weapon wear;copies suppress wear. Bows/throws use the existing ranged wear policy with no new copy durability debit.
7. **Mana,basic fallback and ammunition.** Require the entry table cost to choose the full sequence;recheck current table cost at release. Insufficient entry/release mana produces one basic primary attack,zero skill cost and no copies. Otherwise spend current fractional cost once per released sequence,including a miss or empty melee swing. The future free-air-swing quality-of-life pass is not silently applied. Ordinary successful hits may recover mana afterward;payment and leech are separate. For bows/crossbows validate a quiver at release,create all missiles,then spend one arrow/bolt for the entire series. Throwing consumes one original quantity,without Barbarian Throwing Mastery conservation/critical replenishment. A no-ammunition or failed allocation release spends no mana/quantity and leaves no partial flight set. Failed-windup cancellation pays nothing;completed releases are not refunded.
8. **Ranged travel.** Reuse the existing standard WeaponMissiles straight segment collision/terrain/LOS/height/hostility/serial tie-break and last-collision/pierce services. Each projectile has its own normal hit checks,last collision and captured damage/critical/seed;there is no cast-wide next-hit lease. Use source flags32,velocity24and50frames for this explicit native projection,with first update40ms after release. Ordinary shared pierce supplies each copy's budget and live eligibility;walls/end-of-life stop it. No impact explosion,extra aura or returning blade is added. Standard engine termination removes frame50before another contact;its existing absolute remaining-clock bound is retained. An overdue Mirrored flight at that bound retires without replay. Exact D2R factory geometry,Slow Missiles integration with standard arrows,InitSteps and global hit/RNG ordering remain unverified.
9. **Lifecycle.** Death/deletion,logout/internalization,facet/class change or actual safe-area entry stops future projectile work;timers do not grant recovery,refunds or replay on reconnect. Existing shared death/class-history cleanup removes original mirror flights. Ordinary post-release weapon/gear/training changes retain damage;shared live hit/proc eligibility remains explicit. A nested callback that interrupts a melee sequence stops later copies. Unsupported projectile recipient contexts retire that projectile with a diagnostic instead of guessing a new actor model.
10. **Persistence and admission.** Reuse profile147/version0. Add only optional MirroredMissileSource metadata(rank,ordinal,basic flag,weapon kind,damage seed) to original WeaponFlight;old saves lack it and retain their original meaning. Existing flight IDs,cast IDs,critical/damage payload,motion/pierce/last-collision and clocks remain saved. Validate the new metadata;do not manufacture old mirrors or restart pending attacks. Prepared future-date fixtures isolate serialization and are not a two-second gameplay lifetime extension. Learned left/right/quick binding remains gated until the whole rule is approved,then its real input and automatic clock must be verified. No new timer,client patch,installed deployment or artwork is included.

Final mirror rig/weapon-copy/trail/contact effects and audio,and talent/hotkey icons remain separately missing. Primary UO weapon poses,shared0xF42projectile presentation and0x238audio are identified placeholders. See [native verification and remaining scope](mirrored-world-checkpoint.md). Approval would adopt this complete defined Sanctuary rule,not claim recovered current callbacks or full class/item-property certification.

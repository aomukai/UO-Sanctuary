The old Skeletal Mage strike applied damage immediately while drawing a moving effect. The replacement creates a saved straight flight, captures the target's launch direction, and waits for collision. A moving target can escape the ray; another hostile unit can intercept it. The four contiguous `necromage1..4` rows supply poison, cold, fire and lightning, velocity 14, size 1, a 30-frame lifetime and collision kill. No homing, extra fire splash or poison cloud is invented.

The implementation reuses factory slow/velocity arithmetic, the Q12 direction table, retained source-cell crossings, the ordinary do1 update, shared hitboxes, mitigation, poison/cold states, and the existing intrinsic critical predicate. The current summon formula determines the captured missile rank from the stored summon/mastery snapshot. Elemental endpoints and durations come from the current missile rows. Poison's absent HitShift means zero (a FP256 rate); the other three use HitShift 8. The poison row's physical minimum 0 disables its unusual physical maximum 1280 before a roll. Its length is 100 frames. Cold length uses the missile's three duration tiers. Monster Crit 5 doubles damage/poison rate without doubling duration.

# General weapon packets and Health Link

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**, with approved Sanctuary Health Link and weapon/poison rules first. The old accepted DLL reproduced this receiver losing known components: 5,056 FP256 primary damage, zero of 2,677 expected shared damage, and an explicit missing-components fault. The exact baseline is frozen.

`GearEffects.Strike` now publishes its existing settled non-poison components when the primary life budget is established by those parts, or by those parts plus an explicitly allocated poison rate. Poison remains in the primary total and on its existing scheduler; it is excluded from shared components. A pure prepared-poison packet can therefore carry an empty non-poison context without faulting or sharing damage.

An unexplained extra total or truncated override remains untyped. Its primary damage is retained, and an active Health Link reports the existing missing-components fault. No extra amount is silently assigned to an element, and no valid packet is scaled to fit a different budget. Component preparation, critical handling, before-commit gate, return/leech, cold/poison state, post-hit callbacks and weapon wear retain their original order. Default item suppression still prevents notification; no new item proc or allegiance rule is added.

**Content 1 / Integration 2 / source 2 / native run 1 passed 51 exact results, nine connections, six real basic-attack inputs, three saves and two reloads.** Both accepted builds have zero warnings/errors. Sixteen new controlled cases repeat per boot. Prepared and real factory packets cover ordinary, primary-resisted, recipient-resisted and native-element-immune contexts, retaining any independent magic lane. Other cases cover equal explicit totals, allocated poison, pure poison, unknown extra damage, truncation, zero total, cancellation and pair suppression. Assertions cover exact primary/shared values, original source/recipient, pre-health notification, one commit gate, unchanged resources/wear and the actual prepared-poison scheduler. Unknown cases intentionally restore their exact pre-fault Defiler fixture state for later independent tests.

These controlled cases call the real general receiver and its preparation helper. They do not claim that every modern mapped-NPC attack uses this legacy path, or certify all player-versus-player behavior. Retained Gem Activation tests separately exercise actual elemental strikes, status and persistent item identities; Sacrifice/Vengeance retain their ordered contact checks. Weapon-landed, direct-health-event, leech and eight-class basic-proc fixtures preserve their original scopes. Six authenticated left/right basic attacks and original actor/item/packet reload checks remain in the cohort. Cached source cases are reused without rerunning unchanged reference compilers or a full class sweep.

No corrected native, driver or fixture compilation failure occurred. The successful run has no save-pruning warnings. Twelve intentional scalar-refusal warnings consist of the existing two controls per boot plus the two new unknown-total controls; these are expected negatives, not qualified packets silently failing.

All runtime tables, 240 talent rows/completion flags and 573 named-definition outcomes retain. Installed build, saves, configuration and client remain untouched. Effects/audio and talent/hotkey icons remain separately tracked placeholders. Positive Blade Sentinel NPC flat drain and unclassified legacy overrides remain unsupported contexts; unavailable modern ordering and wider class/PvP qualification are not silently claimed complete.

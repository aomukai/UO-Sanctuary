# Lower Resist checkpoint

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**; approved Sanctuary deviations take precedence. This isolated candidate preserves the installed build and existing saves. No class completion is claimed.

Lower Resist uses the shared learned Necromancer do30 cast and recipient services. Mana is 22 once at release, including a valid empty/resisted area. Source radius is `7 + (rank − 1)` subtiles under the existing world mapping; duration is `500 + 50 × (rank − 1)` frames: 20 seconds at rank 1, 60 seconds at effective rank 21. SC/FCR timing, selected-target tracking, interruption and right/quick bindings remain shared. Source filter3 does not add recipient-to-center LOS.

The four source slots are **fire first**, then lightning, cold and poison. New applications capture each channel’s penalty independently from its underlying native base pool, excluding the existing elite Conviction native offset. Each immune nonplayer/nonhireling channel receives one fifth of its normal penalty, truncated toward zero. Captured values are not divided again at damage time. Physical and magic resistance, outgoing weapon ED and movement/attack rates are unaffected. Existing Conviction mitigation still applies its independently captured penalty; ordinary item pierce follows immunity breaking.

Weaker same-skill recasts reject. Equal-rank recasts retain the original caster and first-slot fire value, restart duration, and rewrite lightning/cold/poison from the new base-resistance context. Higher ranks and different ordinary curses replace the state. Attract and possessed champions reject it. This differs both from Amplify Damage’s single primary stat and Decrepify’s secondary physical stat.

# Learned Leap Attack checkpoint

This is an explicit adaptation of unresolved modern wave emission, travel, overlap and ordering, not a claim of exact executable parity.

The learned cast uses shared saved Leap flight, landing reservations, native movement/input locks, deferred death and offline settlement. It pays ten mana once, uses pinned 175% flight speed and a minimum twelve airborne frames, and has its own beyond-target/free-cell destination search. Adjacent targets still cause a leap. The later contact reuses ordinary player weapon damage, accuracy, block, elemental/item events and geometry; it carries the older successful-hit knockback/raw-hit flags and clears stun after successful completion. Weapon identity and eligibility are rechecked. No target produces the separate short recovery; a missed weapon contact does not repeat the area or payment.

An optional saved attack payload extends the existing version-zero Leap record; ordinary Leap omits it. Restoration validates into a new immutable map before replacing active state. The original three phases and ordinary Leap save format remain compatible. Disconnect/load settle at a safe landing without replaying damage or charging again; external facet movement cleans up without pulling the player back. Death remains deferred while airborne. A dead owner emits no combat after landing, retaining the existing living-owner rule; exact D2R delayed-death area behavior remains uncertain.

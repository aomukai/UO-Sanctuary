The text below is the historical preflight; its no-rollout statement describes commit4de2502, not the current source. Initial aura/priority and modern parity gaps remain open.

1 November 2026, following accepted Blood world commit `00a2907`. Reference remains **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**, with approved Sanctuary deviations first. **Fire still uses its existing learned native actor/controller. No new Fire world actor or factory rollout is enabled by this preflight.** The goal and Necromancer audit remain open.

Reviewed `UnarmedPetWorld`, `IronGolemWorld`, `FireGolemBody`, `FireGolemAura`, `FireGolemDeathBody`, `NecromancerMinion.OnThink`, shared Paladin aura services and existing Fire birth/combat/absorption/aura/death checks before designing the binding. Movement/contact and ordinary owner/load lifecycle can reuse the current ground controller; Fire's independent pulse and delayed death require explicit integration.

The aura damage and weapon-state consumers are already dedicated, but their automatic scheduler is still the historical native `NecromancerMinion.OnThink`: an initial two-second deadline and two-second repeats. Simply stopping native AI, as the other new world actors do, would also stop this automatic aura. Calling the full base `OnThink` would reintroduce native combat/recovery. Port the scheduling deliberately and call the existing `PulseHolyFire`; do not duplicate aura formulas, damage or native AI.

The complete unchanged older-source `SKILL_ComputePeriodicRate`, periodic installer `sub_6FD13260` and monster callback `D2GAME_MONSTERS_AiFunction09_6FD13470` now have [dedicated executable evidence](../tools/reference/periodic-skill-clock-source-evidence.json): **8,008 clock,384 installation and24,576 callback contexts (32,968 total)**, with clean ASan/UBSan. In the bounded nonoverflow domain, the clock is `frame + max(5, delay)`, not global frame-bucket alignment. Installation preserves separate selected-skill versus explicit-skill payloads and replaces the corresponding queued event. Callback guards distinguish selected living aura from a state-list skill/rank; a supplied handler return0 does not itself stop renewal. Formula, skill/state/stat-list, handler and queue storage are declared services, not execution of the modern engine.

The existing [Holy Fire source probe](../tools/reference/fire-golem-aura-source-evidence.json) now composes that complete clock body with complete Do66 and recipient callback bodies instead of declaring the clock service. **All384 previous outcomes remain byte-for-byte unchanged**, including self application before town, nonplayer mana bypass and51-frame self-state lifetime from current perdelay50. Other formula/world/stat services remain explicit. Current executable initial aura activation, first deadline and event priority remain unverified; the repeated-event proof does not determine summon startup.

Static review of the complete pinned monster event dispatcher shows periodic skills exempt from its ordinary freeze suppression. That dispatcher is hashed in the evidence and was **read, not executed** by this harness. The future scheduler must not silently tie aura progression to successful movement/AI actions or stop it merely because the actor is frozen. Offline/parked actors must keep existing no-attack/no-catch-up behavior and ordinary lease expiry.

No Fire ground actor is enabled yet, so this does not describe its current native startup. Add a simultaneous-end/think fixture and preserve the scheduled due event before enabling the new binding. Exact modern event priority remains a separate acceptance gap.

Fire has its own G3 A1speed256/contact10/end17, velocity10/run10/WLspeed192 and captured Mastery movement. Keep those distinct from Clay/Blood/skeleton and original-item Iron. The optional monster elemental/noRatio branch and mode-entry RNG remain an existing source-versus-current reference discrepancy; do not invent an extra fire roll.

The new ground controller must detach/cancel its contact when that transition begins, avoid regeneration/movement/aura work on a retiring actor, preserve the existing original source/owner and let the dedicated saved retirement timer finish. A Thorns death during contact must not continue a later source-frame catch-up on that dead body. Reloading a retired body must not register it for ordinary ground combat. Reuse replacement/absorption/death consumers; do not replace this with immediate deletion or a new caster proxy.

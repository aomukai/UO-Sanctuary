# Poison Nova source preparation

Mechanical reference: D2R Reign of the Warlock **3.3.93847**. Approved Sanctuary deviations take precedence. This preparation changes no runtime handler or imported game data and does not certify the current provisional Nova implementation.

The [emitter proof](../tools/reference/poison-nova-emitter-source-evidence.json), reproduced by `python3 tools/probe_poison_nova_emitter.py --check`, composes the complete older do22 Nova caller, 64-offset ring emitter and missile factory against the current row. Across 72 contexts it attempts 768 allocations and creates 384 children. Failed allocations do not terminate later attempts. Each child retains the exact integer radial aim, origin, original owner, skill/rank and 30-frame lifetime. Current velocity 12 becomes 2,304 FP8. The row has no CanSlow flag, so the supplied Slow Missiles state does not slow its movement. Progressive missile selection, constant table-velocity lookup, world allocation/path/stat and packet services remain declared boundaries.

The [packet proof](../tools/reference/poison-nova-damage-source-evidence.json), reproduced by `python3 tools/probe_poison_nova_damage.py --check`, executes complete creator, skill damage/length/mastery getters, stat writer and impact roll over 12,240 contexts. It covers all 255 ranks, four feasible hard-synergy totals, three poison-masteries, supplied weapon/item/origin values and critical controls. HitShift 4 stores a poison rate; the length remains 50 frames. Ten-percent hard synergies apply before poison mastery, with integer endpoint truncation. Weapon/origin poison and physical damage and critical do not enter SrcDam0. The skill packet has poison count 0, so its length must not be divided by an invented count. At rank 1 without synergy/mastery, the raw rates are 256–464 FP256 per frame, lasting 50 frames; the displayed total is derived separately.

This is a 64-child missile spell, rather than a single expanding area pulse. Its current generic do1/Size1/contact-kill/LastCollide/four-frame NextHit path must be composed with saved rays and the shared poison-rate delivery/clock. The modern NextHit cast/caster identity remains uncertain, as recorded for Teeth. Poison application, resistance and duration scaling, initial health delivery, ongoing poison ordering, credit/death, lifecycle, resource payment, actual inputs and reloads require native verification. Current path, global phase and graphical-client parity remain unproven.

Reuse the saved spell controller, source factory/vector/steps, existing poison packets and durable credit consumers where their contracts match. Do not transfer Bone Spear's direct magic health hit or invent poison length from a total-damage field. Keep existing mechanic uncertainties named and continue unaffected work. Final poisonNova flight/cast/contact effects/audio and the talent/hotkey icon remain separately listed placeholders; no image generation blocks this preparation.

The [user-approved potion clarification](../tools/reference/poison-spell-potion-deviation.json) requires the four elemental additions alongside each pure-poison spell tick, with buff eligibility at that tick. Preserve this separate Sanctuary rule when composing the raw poison rate and shared clock. Activation after poisoning and expiry before the poison ends need explicit tests, along with immunity, cure/replacement, attribution and reload boundaries. No tick implementation is claimed by this source preparation.

Implementation review notes: `PreparedPoison.Commit` and `TimedEffects.PoisonFrames` already carry FP256 rate and duration into the shared 40ms poison clock. `PoisonStates.Advance` handles ordinary clocks; `AdvanceEquipped` combines poison with NPC item replenishment before lethal resolution. Reuse these contracts instead of adding an unrelated damage timer. `PoisonStates.Drain` deliberately bypasses ordinary hit recovery/interruption and applies T29 Protection; a second independent bonus drain would risk applying Protection twice. Compose and verify the elemental tick additions with one reduction boundary. Preserve the independent poison-length lane and spell attribution, and explicitly review zero-rate immunity, cure/replacement and removed-caster cases. Do not fabricate a positive poison rate merely to keep an elemental bonus clock alive. No implementation decision for those edge cases is claimed here.

Reuse the existing effect scheduler, keep natural regeneration/poison-status visibility independent, and clear the exposure on cure.

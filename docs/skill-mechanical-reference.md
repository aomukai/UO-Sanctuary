# Skill mechanical reference and precedence

The class-skill pass uses **Diablo II: Resurrected 3.3.93847 — Reign of the Warlock**. This pin is intentional; future patches do not change the implementation baseline automatically. Sanctuary's approved decisions take precedence over the reference. The [machine-readable pin](../data/Sanctuary/skill-reference.json) records the exact edition, source commits, comparison hashes, Warlock identities and limitations.

## Sanctuary decisions retained

The [kickoff brief](../Britannia-Diablo-Project-Kickoff.md), [foundation rules](balance-foundations.md), [combat timing](combat-timing.md), [allied movement](allied-movement.md), [class histories](t39-class-histories.md), [wardrobe transactions](t40-wardrobe-transactions.md), [pet persistence](death-and-pets.md) and [Teleport implementation](t33-teleport.md) remain authoritative where they differ from D2R.

This preserves server-owned UO tile movement/collision and existing action/recovery timings; friendly/neutral/player targeting restrictions; rank-20 **allocated** caps with separate effective gear ranks; saved independent class histories and per-class bindings; the specific starter dagger's Teeth grant; corpse access/harvesting/Rest in Peace rules and retained corpse loot; and class summon parking/ownership. Existing potion, food, rune and named-gear combat rules remain shared consumers. Teleport retains its approved Sanctuary learning, scroll/tome, targeting and timing behavior. The older equipment/foundation reference pins are not globally replaced by this class reference.

**Approved Zeal deviation:** the user tested the pinned build below two mana and observed that Zeal stops without attacking. Sanctuary instead performs an ordinary attack when an otherwise valid Zeal attempt is unaffordable. Held input repeats ordinary attacks and resumes Zeal when two mana are available, retaining the Zeal binding. Ordinary attack timing, hit/damage and cancellation apply, without a Zeal series or mana debit. This approval is specific to Zeal; see the [firsthand report and decision](../tools/reference/zeal-low-mana-deviation.json).

Skill geometry is translated through the existing tile footprint and LOS APIs. Bone Armor absorbs physical damage before resistance; elemental damage bypasses it. Hard-point synergies use allocated ranks, while ordinary skill scaling uses effective ranks. Fractional mana costs retain 1/256 units. Ordinary curse replacement, Attract's non-overwrite rule and immunity-breaking penalties are explicit consumers.

The explicit `SkillGeometry` port mapping uses two D2 subtiles per UO tile, with distances and missile velocities converted together; Corpse Explosion additionally converts its half-subtile radius parameter. This is **provisional, not an additional approved deviation**. New geometry choices requiring sign-off include Teeth's tile fan angle and next-hit behavior, the child-cloud footprint of Poison Explosion, barrier footprints and some summon projectile timings. Their handlers are not evidence of exact D2R parity. Several AI/species exceptions, returned-damage events and summon/barrier difficulty rules also remain open. See the per-skill [roadmap](class-skill-roadmap.md). Unsupported Iron Golem and Revive remain unavailable rather than being replaced with generic spells.

Bone Wall/Prison use the pinned `bonewall` Normal base and skill synergy/lifetime cells. Supplementary legacy [maker behavior](https://github.com/ThePhrozenKeep/D2MOO/blob/0c0bbd1e6bef9181a3da1c046523af622a925416/source/D2Game/src/MISSILES/MissMode.cpp) and [barrier AI](https://github.com/ThePhrozenKeep/D2MOO/blob/0c0bbd1e6bef9181a3da1c046523af622a925416/source/D2Game/src/AI/AiThink.cpp) inform growth and expiry. The provisional Prison raster retains twelve logical segments and attaches four connector cells to their health. Maker `Range` expression handling, exact growth cadence, inherited level and shared-world difficulty are unresolved. Native arrows and the existing monster firebolt are intercepted; this does not establish every projectile/area-effect exception. Existing skeleton bodies and invisible UO collision art are explicit placeholders.

Blood Golem's healing uses a single budget, the target's drain effectiveness, the source owner share, and unused-healing transfers between recipients. This follows the supplementary legacy `EventFunc23` and Blizzard's summoning guide; direct comparison against the pinned D2R executable is still pending. Fire Golem's passive stat positions differ from other golems, so consumers select named stats instead of assuming fixed slots. Replacement explosions use its `calc2`–`calc4` cells.

Dim Vision retains an existing chase or flee decision, stops that motion on an obstruction, and limits new acquisition to melee range while suppressing special actions. The native direct-step adapter retains Sanctuary movement pacing and terrain checks. Motion survives save/reload; curse expiration releases AI control. The legacy guide supports this interpretation, but species-specific and actual-client acceptance remain open. Terror has no invented caster/target-level gate. Flee persistence when other ordinary curses replace Terror still requires the pinned engine comparison.

The three cold armors use separate events: physical melee damage for Frozen Armor, attempted melee for Shiver Armor, and a missile's source `ReturnFire` eligibility for Chilling Armor. They share one exclusive armor slot and coexist with Energy Shield. The pinned tables define defense, duration, retaliation and synergy values; legacy [SkillSor event functions](https://github.com/ThePhrozenKeep/D2MOO/blob/0c0bbd1e6bef9181a3da1c046523af622a925416/source/D2Game/src/SKILLS/SkillSor.cpp) explain the distinct events and shield channel order. Source/impact modifier timing and every projectile exception are still acceptance questions.

Energy Shield absorbs eligible damage before flat reduction/resistance and spends fractional mana in physical, fire, lightning, cold and magic order. Efficiency uses allocated Telekinesis ranks; poison remains outside that channel list. Mana depletion removes the shield. Special drain fields are absent from the shared damage representation, and native ignore-armor calls bypass this shield consumer; both remain unsupported. Telekinesis implements hostile-monster damage and source-chance knockback; its remote item/object interactions and forced hit recovery remain unsupported rather than being approximated with generic interactions.

The immutable legacy [sequence table](https://github.com/ThePhrozenKeep/D2MOO/blob/0c0bbd1e6bef9181a3da1c046523af622a925416/source/D2Common/src/DataTbls/SequenceTbls.cpp) has 19 points and an action at point 7. Sanctuary's existing ally policy supplies friendly targets; charged/oskill delivery, special attacks and client acceptance remain separate.

## Source gaps and nonblocking questions

1. Preserve every original formula cell. Fire Wall has a scoped legacy-compiler-backed adapter; Inferno's synergy is balanced and verified. Any other malformed expression still needs compiler evidence before enabling a corrected interpretation.
2. Confirm tile-space footprints, source next-hit delays and moving-target collision behavior through controlled reference comparisons. Do not treat a placeholder effect as collision evidence.
3. Confirm pet difficulty scaling in Sanctuary's shared world. The new summon pass currently uses the source **Normal** pet bases; a shared-world rule for pets entering Nightmare/Hell areas is not yet settled.
4. Iron Golem needs a metal-item/ownership/consumption contract and full retained item properties. Revive needs source-preserving species/abilities and corpse metadata that survives deletion/reload. Both are genuine dependencies.
5. Decide the earned level-99 prefix for Warlock/Witch during that class's pass. No title was invented in this checkpoint.
6. Final art remains separate work: [effects](skill-effects.md), [icons](skill-icons.md). Image generation, client integration and gameplay verification are distinct states.

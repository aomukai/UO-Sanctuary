# Clay Golem uses shared skeleton damage and defensive affixes

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**, with approved Sanctuary deviations first. This increment follows `bfab5bb`. It implements the approved Clay damage curve and hard-point defensive unlocks within the scope below. Necromancer and the full class goal remain unfinished.

Before implementation, checked Skeleton Warrior creation/raw damage, captured summon skill contexts, NPC physical/critical/item/Enchant/debuff consumers, Clay exact birth/contact/world/slow handlers, native resistance seeds, encounter Stone Skin/Magic Resistant and saved actor layouts. `ClayGolemUpgrades` binds those existing services rather than creating another damage formula, damage receiver or affix table.

Clay raw endpoints/percentage now use `SkeletonStats.WeaponBase` at its captured effective Clay rank, with captured effective **Golem Mastery replacing Skeleton Mastery**. The previous innate Clay weapon and passive Fire-synergy damage are replaced. Capture rules for other birth resources remain unchanged. Existing damage auras/debuffs, original-item contributions, Enchant, intrinsic criticals, fractional rolls, prepared contact, reactive slow and action timing retain their shared consumers. Native whole-number display/fallback and source FP256 rolls keep their different existing rounding boundaries. At rank 4 / Normal/no Mastery, the new exclusive range is 273..546 FP256 rather than the previous 1049..2623; this intentional baseline replacement is not disguised as an additive buff.

10 hard points grant Stone Skin: double the pet’s base profile defense and apply the existing physical resistance modifier. 20 hard points also grant Magic Resistant through the existing elemental resistance policy. Soft points do not unlock either. The ordered affix helper receives the current host native resistance seeds/equipment, with Stone Skin before Magic Resistant. Existing already-immune and two-immunity restrictions are preserved. Magic Resistant does not add magic or poison resistance. External defense/curse/receiver behavior remains in its existing consumer.

Training/respec/relearn changes affixes on the original pet. It does not recapture Clay/Golem Mastery damage ranks, copy Skeleton Mastery, heal, refill, replace/reorder the actor or change its RNG/attack clock. Current learned and exact historical Clay subtypes use their existing fractional packet/world path. Original plain `NecromancerMinion` Clay actors retain their original type, native life/controller and whole-number fallback while gaining the replacement curve and live affixes; their complete source-frame/FP migration remains open.

# Eldritch Blast: proposed complete Sanctuary rule

**Approved by the user on2026-11-15 JST.** [Exact decision](../tools/reference/eldritch-blast-world-rule-approval.json). The body below is the original approved proposal; implementation/acceptance status is recorded separately.

**Original proposal.** Mechanical reference remains **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. This is a prepared world implementation, not a learned handler or a claim that protected current code has been recovered. The earlier complete Bind Demon approval is independent and remains in force.

The owned skill384/state221/missile721 tables establish magic damage, 50% per hard Blade Warp/Hex Purge point, a duration of `1000 + 50 × hard Psychic Ward` frames, raw range5, period30, flat5 life/mana return and fractional mana `(10 + effective rank) / 2`. Unused growth parameters do not override formulas. The retained current do165 capture still contains only92 readable bytes out of1198; this work performs no new game/process reads and does not substitute installed3.4 data.

The user's [research notes](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gtM3sQ1gzo8kpmTmiJ8CQAiqp7LLu0SeaWE6fUdVg6Y/edit?tab=t.0), also available as the [authors' compiled/tested notes](https://upload3.inven.co.kr/upload/2026/03/02/bbs/WarlockSkillsNotesandFunctionality.pdf), corroborate1.2-second pulses and Hex refresh despite magic immunity. Their older growing leech percentage conflicts with the pinned flat5 fields and is not adopted. The supplied [datamined skill page](https://diabloforfans.com/skill/eldritch-blast) identifies3.3.93847 and corroborates the periodic self-buff. These sources support that broad behavior; the following unresolved boundaries are explicit design adaptations.

1. **Cast and capture.** Use the shared Warlock SC/FCR self-cast, requiring its current fractional mana cost at entry and release. Spend once on a successful release, including an empty area; interrupted or underfunded casts create no state. Capture effective Blast rank, hard damage/duration synergies, magic mastery and Magical Penetration potion/masteries at release. Recasting pays again and replaces the old state and pulse grid; it does not stack. Exact current startup28 and capture timing are unverified. The prepared checkpoint tests paid release; learned SC/left/right/quick dispatch remains a subsequent acceptance step.
2. **Lifetime and cadence.** First pulse after30frames/**1.2seconds**, then every30frames until the captured40–80-second expiry. No immediate pulse. Expiry wins at an equal deadline. A late tick performs at most one pulse and advances to the next original grid deadline. Offline, internal-map and actual safe-area periods consume time without damage, refresh, recovery, RNG or replay. Returning to the world resumes the remaining duration. Death, deletion, leaving the class or losing the skill clears the state. Safe-area entry does not heal or reset anything.
3. **Recipients and geometry.** Pulse around the caster's live position and facet. Reuse raw range5 through the shared source-circle projection (**2.5 UO tiles**), height, hostility, safe-region and center-to-target LOS checks. Process by serial. Only living, unblessed enemies with an active Bane, Siphon or Purge target Hex qualify. A Hex may belong to another caster; its ownership stays unchanged. Ordinary supported native monsters only; unsupported class-pet/PvP/return contexts produce an explicit fault before that pulse changes targets. No damage against unhexed enemies. The native circle/order/LOS policy is an adaptation.
4. **Hex maintenance.** Refresh the existing attachment before damage or immunity checks, using its captured original full duration. Preserve creator, rank, numeric penalties, Purge packet, seed and remaining charges. Never refill, replace, transfer or resurrect a Hex. Bane/Siphon now save an optional refresh duration; historical saves without it load unchanged and expire normally. A pulse encountering that unknown historical duration refuses with a diagnostic until the Hex expires or is reapplied. Purge already stores its duration. Do not derive historical duration from current gear/training. Group-conflict states refuse rather than picking a Hex silently.
5. **Damage packet.** One saved source-compatible RNG stream per captured state; draw one FP256 magic packet shared by all recipients in a nonempty qualified pulse. No draw for an empty or suspended pulse. Apply captured magic mastery once to the endpoints, then captured potion additions through the existing spell helper. Recipient defenses, resistance, immunity and caster piercing are read at impact through existing services. No weapon damage, accuracy roll, shield block, dodge, critical, hit recovery, stun or knockback. Missile721 is represented by a cosmetic nova; it is not an additional damaging actor or an unverified travelling collision simulation.
6. **Periodic events.** Use the existing `SkillDamageKind.Periodic` boundary, as Hurricane does. Pulses do not trigger direct-hit Purge reactions or direct-hit Health Link sharing, and do not apply weapon Hexes/on-strike effects. Existing ordinary damage/death credit and kill services retain their behavior. This event choice is explicitly unverified for current D2R and requires approval alongside the rest of the rule.
7. **Recovery.** Recover flat5% of the post-mitigation **magic component**, capped by actual FP256 life lost to that target. Added potion elements, overkill and zero/absorbed magic grant no magic leech. Reuse the shared weapon-leech calculation's difficulty divisors and monster drain effectiveness, with fractional precision and ordinary owner-resource caps. No item leech bonus or victim-mana deduction. Apply recovery only to the living caster; no recovery for a dead/deleted owner. Exact current basis/order and leech reductions remain adaptations. If a callback changes ownership/state/world context, stop subsequent recipients; already-completed damage/payment is retained without refund or replay.
8. **Persistence and assets.** Save immutable captured numbers/modifiers, RNG and original absolute deadlines in new optional profile252/version0. Existing profile versions remain unchanged. Reject malformed/duplicate snapshots atomically. No automatic timer or learned route is enabled while pending. After approval, use an existing shared tick, add ordinary death cleanup, and verify learned input. Shared `0x375A` contact/aura, `0x37CC` cosmetic nova and `0x1E5` sound are identified placeholders. Final `eldritchblastnova`, `surge_hex_explode`, `eldritchblastperiodic`, `warlock_psychic_cast_1_no_hex`, associated audio and talent/hotkey icons remain separately missing.

See [prepared verification and limits](eldritch-blast-world-checkpoint.md). Approval would adopt this whole defined rule; it would not certify every class, unsupported native target, exact D2R code parity or final graphical assets.

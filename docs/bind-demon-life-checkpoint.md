# Bound demons with existing life buffs

**Accepted: C1/I2/S2/N2; 30 native markers, nine authenticated connections, three boots/saves and two reloads.**

Prepared binding now accepts an ordinary native creature already carrying Battle Orders, Oak Sage, or both. It captures the unbuffed Normal life maximum, applies the existing bound-life cap, and retains the live buffs once above that cap. The original buff source, supplier and deadline remain intact. Current life keeps its percentage; mana and stamina are neither refilled nor rewritten.

`BattleOrders.NativeLifeBase256` observes the input to the existing native maximum callback. It checks the returned maximum against the shared resource calculation, without reversing a rounded/capped result or temporarily removing effects. `MonsterVariantBaselines.RememberNormal` accepts that explicit life input for a new or old null-life Normal baseline. A conflicting saved baseline, an unknown Strength modifier, or an opaque maximum composition refuses explicitly. Higher-born creatures still require their saved original Normal baseline. This is a prepared native resource adapter, **not recovered D2R binder HP ordering**.

The focused native cohort retains original bound-host behavior, bound aura providers, original Revive facet changes and the existing NPC resource controls. New cases cover:

- Actual Battle Orders at 35% before binding, with fresh, cached and old null-life Normal baselines. An original 50,000-life creature reaches the native 65,000 cap; binding correctly stores 50,000, caps at 20,000, and then reaches 27,000 with Battle Orders.
- Actual Oak Sage at 30%, alone and combined with Battle Orders. Bound maxima are 26,000 and 33,000. Both leases retain their original metadata; removing either buff preserves wounds.
- A 12,345-life original whose buffed native maximum loses fractional precision. The bound FP8 maximum is calculated from the original base rather than inverted from that display.
- Normal, Nightmare and Hell rescaling, parking, two native reloads, and genuine buff removal/reapplication after each reload. Original identity, saved base, relative wounds and native resource balances persist.
- Unknown native Strength/maximum composition and conflicting historical baselines refuse without ownership or resource changes. The scoped observation restores after failure.

# Revive behavior replacements

Design checkpoint, 2026-10-30. Reference: D2R Reign of the Warlock **3.3.93847**, with the [approved native-behavior policy](../tools/reference/revive-native-behavior-policy.json) taking precedence. This document describes decisions and intended adapters. It does not enable learned Revive or certify special attacks as implemented.

**Scope correction:** replacing UO monster spells with D2 spells is a [separate game-wide task](monster-spell-conversion.md), covering all monsters in their wild form as well. The caster rows below identify requirements for that shared conversion. Revive reuses the resulting monster repertoire with its own eligibility/lifecycle rules; it must not become a parallel implementation of monster spell conversion. Compatible non-spell behavior may still be retained under the approved policy.

Keep the original creature's identity and compatible movement, attack selection and presentation. Replace conflicting mechanics individually, reusing existing D2 combat services. A missing ability adapter must have a specific reason; it must not stall unrelated skills.

| Behavior | Decision | Implementation boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Following, hostile acquisition and ordinary melee | Keep the accepted native AI adapters. | Existing following, proactive Guard acquisition and source weapon-base acceptance remain valid within their documented scopes. This is not exact D2 AI/RNG parity. |
| Dragon breath presentation | Keep compatible animation, facing, sound and effect as identified placeholders. | Recheck owner activity, map, hostility and target permission at dispatch and every delayed callback. Existing parked-breath cancellation does not establish active damage acceptance. |
| Dragon breath damage | Replace native current-life scaling with an explicit creature spell packet. | Native FireBreath computes current Hits times a scalar, with a 200-damage cap. Revive/Mastery life must not incidentally become spell damage. Use an authored difficulty/level spell profile and shared D2 mitigation; do not borrow an arbitrary player skill rank or turn the proposed dragon analogue into an approved counterpart. Damage/rate/range numbers remain unassigned until supported by the role profile. |
| Generic mage damage selection | Adapt per authored role rather than retain the entire UO MageAI spell bag. | Use the existing CreatureAttack Spell fields for explicit damage, element, skill/missile and timing where established. Reuse shared projectile/field geometry and damage services where compatible. Missing fields mean an unsupported role, not zero damage or an invented rank. |
| Generic UO Heal, Greater Heal and Cure | Remove through the global monster spell conversion. | This applies to ordinary wild monsters as well as their revived versions. Do not substitute automatic potion consumption or suppress existing D2-specific healing such as Prayer/Holy Bolt. No runtime spell selector has been changed by this design checkpoint. |
| Special healer or resurrector monsters | Support as explicitly authored monster abilities. | The user approves special mobs that heal other monsters, alongside the existing lich resurrection concept. Give each role its own eligibility, targeting, timing and interruption rules. This is not blanket permission for every mage to heal, nor a decision that Revive inherits resurrection. |
| UO Strength/Dexterity/Intelligence drains and unrelated curses | Replace only through a documented D2-compatible role effect. | Do not directly retain Weaken/Clumsy/Feeblemind/Curse just because their native classes exist. Select the intended combat effect per role, then use the matching existing curse/state machinery with explicit duration and stacking. No universal replacement curse or numeric values are selected yet. |
| Automatic dispel, arbitrary helper summons and corpse resurrection | Require a separate role decision. | Native ownership already changes some auto-dispel behavior. Do not bypass those guards, delete class minions as ordinary UO summons, create unbudgeted helpers, or consume corpses outside shared reservation rules. D2's historical Revive description excludes raising other monsters; the newly approved special-monster design does not silently override that inheritance rule. |
| UO path-failure teleport | Review separately from ordinary walking. | Retain native pathfinding where compatible; do not silently authorize a teleport through walls or across facets. A reviewed movement adapter must obey destination legality and persistent-minion lifecycle. |
| Equipment, loot and native ability clocks | Keep physical gear lootable; apply combat effects independently. | Existing immutable capture is not live proc/stat attachment. Review clocks per ability; an archived timer need not be restored merely because it was captured. |

## Source findings to retain

- The accepted native tree is pinned by [native melee acceptance](../tools/reference/revive-native-melee-evidence.json). Its `Mobiles/Abilities/Fire Breath/FireBreath.cs` uses current health for damage, includes delayed effect/damage callbacks and supplies a native 30–45 second cooldown. Those native values identify the conflict; they are not approved D2 replacement values.
- `Mobiles/AI/MageAI.cs` selects a broad UO damage/curse/mana-drain spell set and can cure or heal a controlled creature. Simply retaining AI_Mage is therefore not complete D2 spell conversion. `Dragon.cs` uses AI_Mage plus FireBreath; `Lich.cs` uses AI_Mage and native spell skills.
- The current creature catalogue labels Dragon's `megademon3` mapping a **proposed creative analogue with stat application disabled**. Lich has no D2 reference. Neither constitutes a completed relative-strength classification.
- The complete older Revive release in pinned D2MOO `SkillNec.cpp` calls summon passive setup, owner registration and target-node setup, but does **not** call `D2GAME_SetSummonResistance_6FD0C2E0`. Skeleton/golem release paths do call it. Blizzard's [historical summoning page](https://classic.battle.net/diablo2exp/skills/necromancer-summoning.shtml) nevertheless describes Summon Resist helping Revives. Record this as a reference conflict; do not add the bonus by assumption or claim current-build exclusion is verified. The [2022 player bug report](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/d2r/t/revive-erroneously-displays-summon-resist-as-a-synergy/130119) is supplementary testimony, not a 3.3.93847 runtime test.
- `D2Common_10214` is collision-footprint restoration in `D2Common/src/Path/Path.cpp`; it is not a regeneration initializer. `D2GAME_UpdateSummonAI_6FC401F0` in `Targets.cpp` links a target node; its name alone does not prove a replacement AI implementation. Source regeneration remains a separate stat/event consumer, not established by either call.

## Next implementation and acceptance

Finish a coherent ordinary-melee learned Revive route using the accepted original-actor/lifecycle services, with explicit unsupported-role reasons. Compose eligibility, automatic capture, placement, payment, resources, regeneration, respec and facet scaling; do not represent the prepared activation API as a learned handler. Then extend reviewed roles one at a time without replacing the original NPC object.

For a special-attack adapter, verify friendly/hostile targeting, blocked line of sight, a target moving or dying during windup, damage once through D2 resistance/absorb, immunity, owner logout/death/facet change, and actual save/reload. Check that life bonuses do not change a replacement breath packet. Test ordinary wild creatures/pets as controls before changing shared native hooks.

Missing effects/audio and talent/hotkey icons remain in their separate registers. Existing native visuals may serve as explicitly named placeholders; no image generation is needed for mechanical acceptance.

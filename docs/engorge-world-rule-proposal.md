# Complete Engorge action and recipient rule

Prepared Sanctuary adaptation against **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. Owned numerical suppliers and tooltip meanings are authenticated in [the supplier checkpoint](engorge-suppliers-checkpoint.md). Current do161's seven retained fragments remain unreadable. This proposal defines the missing world, startup, state-writer and drain contract rather than claiming to recover that callback. **Learned admission remains gated until this complete rule is approved.**

1. **Corpse selection.** Use the existing corpse eligibility, access, LOS, floor-height and one-consumer reservation rules, preserving their exclusions for player/summoned/owned, shattered, carved, harvested, consumed and unavailable corpses. Require a live connected Warlock/Witch outside actual safe areas, an available Engorge rank, and a corpse within 19 native tiles outside town. Clicking ground acquires the eligible corpse nearest the cursor within the already-approved two-tile helper, with serial ties. A direct corpse selection retains that corpse's identity. Revalidate at release. This ground tolerance and world projection are explicit adaptations; opaque raw `aurarangecalc=1` is not silently relabeled as a recipient radius.
2. **Recipients.** At release, use all eligible original learned Goatman/Tainted/Defiler bodies in the shared saved creation order. Each must be alive, same facet, within 19 tiles of its owner, outside town, active and unparked, permitted by its owner, and backed by its original fault-free controller and native demon registration. No extra owner-to-pet LOS requirement. Unsupported/legacy actors, retired pets, other facets, offline/parked pets and converted equipment contexts are excluded. Bind Demon receives no invented host; add it only when its own actor is qualified. No eligible recipient means no payment or corpse consumption.
3. **Action and payment.** Reuse the player's shared SC/FCR clock and normal overlap, form, incapacitation and resource checks. Require enough mana to begin; spend none during windup. Movement—including moving away and back—weapon changes, explicit stop, interruption, invalid owner or missing corpse cancel the pending work. At release, recapture current Engorge rank and hard Blood Oath points, validate resources and roster, reserve and consume the corpse, then pay once and apply recipients. The row has no added local cooldown. A completed native callback is never rolled back: if an unexpected later recipient failure occurs, retain the consumed corpse, paid mana and already-applied effects, log the refusal and never replay the action. A failed precommit releases nothing and costs no mana. Starting windup still occupies its shared action cycle.
4. **Healing.** For each recipient, restore the captured `ln34` percentage of its **current maximum life**, using FP8 source arithmetic and the missing-life cap. Thus rank 1 heals 30%; rank 20 heals 49%. This immediate healing occurs only when a corpse is successfully fed. Do not rewrite the summon's birth values or maximum life. Protected corpse loot stays in the consumed corpse.
5. **Timed state.** Install one captured Engorge payload per recipient. A recast replaces that payload and restarts its duration, including a weaker recast; bonuses never stack. Rank 1 lasts 125 source frames / 5 seconds, rank 20 lasts 1,550 frames / 62 seconds. Duration is an absolute UTC interval, active from application inclusive until expiry exclusive. Subsequent gear/training changes do not alter an existing state. Saving/loading, parking, logout and facet travel do not restart it or heal the pet. Death/deletion removes the body and its state. Existing parking/difficulty policies stay in force.
6. **Shared stat consumers.** Add the captured flat armor before existing defense-percent effects and Berserk overrides. Add 5 physical-resistance points in the existing resistance pool before curse/conviction/immunity and mitigation rules. Add captured skill attackrate—15 at rank 1, capped at 35 from rank 21—to the shared attack-rate pool. Movement speed is unchanged. All existing slows and final speed clamps still apply; an already-captured animation keeps its clock. This also feeds the Tainted's existing attack-mode Fire Ball clock; the nonattacking Defiler does not acquire a weapon attack.
7. **Recovery.** Capture flat armor as 25 per hard Blood Oath point and raw `hpregen` as 5 per hard Blood Oath point. Interpret that raw regeneration as an additive FP8 amount per existing 40 ms source recovery frame. Reuse the summon recovery path and its poison, healing prevention, activity and parking checks; install no second timer. Bulk intervals count only frame instants inside the buff's actual lifetime. Skipped/offline intervals do not grant recovery on return. This is an explicit stat-writer adaptation, not an invented percentage of maximum life.
8. **Life steal adaptation.** The owned tooltip calls `ln21` a percentage, but the older ordinary NPC allocator treats its `lifedrainmin/maxdam` fields as flat amounts. Use the **tooltip percentage** for Engorge: 15% at rank 1, 53% at rank 20. On the actual Goatman's successful ordinary melee commitment, use its settled physical component clipped to the victim's current life. Reuse `WeaponLeech.Calculate`, including six-bit percentage precision, the target's drain effectiveness and current Normal/Nightmare/Hell divisor (1/2/3). Heal only the actual attacking demon, capped at missing life, once before victim HP commitment. No owner gear leech inheritance, owner healing, mana theft or extra attack proc. Elemental-only, suppressed, missed/blocked/dodged, ranged, periodic and expired/offline/inactive contacts give no Engorge leech. Tainted spells and the nonattacking Defiler do not leech. Positive old raw-drain packets are explicitly refused rather than silently combined with this percentage rule. Existing Life Tap remains independent.
9. **Save compatibility.** Store the optional payload in each original demon's existing JSON body state. Validate captured ranks/numbers, owner and exact application/expiry interval atomically. Old saves omit the field and gain no Engorge state. No persistence ID, serialization version, replayed cast or separate buff timer is added. An expired payload can remain inert in the body until replacement/deletion; every consumer checks its deadline.
10. **Assets and limits.** Reuse explicitly identified shared cast and heal/buff placeholders. Final corpse-transfer, buff and sound effects are separately missing from tree/hotkey icons. Prepared/native checks qualify the proposed behavior and original saves; they do not establish hidden do161 equivalence, future Bind Demon behavior, all PvP/global-world/client behavior or full class completion. Activation needs this complete adaptation decision under the pinned-reference requirement.

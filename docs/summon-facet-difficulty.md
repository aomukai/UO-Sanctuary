# Summon difficulty across facets

Approved Sanctuary deviation, 25 October 2026: a facet's explicit Normal/Nightmare/Hell designation supplies summon difficulty. When the owner enters a facet with a different difficulty, **existing summons rescale** and **preserve their current life percentage**. This takes precedence over treating D2R's game-wide difficulty as permanently fixed at summon creation.

Specific Nightmare/Hell facet assignments will be designated later. No facet names or assignments have been invented. The existing native location/map hook now resolves those designations for the five persistent Necromancer summon families.

Recompute difficulty-dependent actor stats from retained summoning context. Rescaling does not count as another cast or summon, does not recharge mana or restart lifetime, and must not recollect an already-created missile's weapon damage from later gear. Preserve FP256 health precision and the existing ownership, parking, following, teleport and death rules. Injured transitions in both directions, fractional rounding, parked/offline summons and two save/reloads pass for skeletons, skeletal mages and clay/blood/fire golems.

**Implementation status:** implemented and verified for Raise Skeleton, Raise Skeletal Mage, Clay Golem, Blood Golem and Fire Golem. Existing summons refresh life, attack, defense, weapon damage and resistances from retained ranks and the pinned difficulty columns. New summons use the owner facet immediately. Existing Battle Orders keeps its supplier, rank and expiry; resource/regeneration updates reuse its established consumer. Current life is scaled with integer arithmetic, floored once to FP256 (minimum1/256 for a positive living balance); a same-difficulty notification leaves it untouched.

The new `SanctuarySummonDifficulties` version0 ledger loads before mobile stat reconstruction. The existing summon serialization chains are unchanged, and older summons without ledger entries load as Normal. Logout to the Internal map does not assign a new difficulty. Parked summons rescale from their owner's playable facet, and resume/login catches a missed transition. The existing native hook also retains its map-survey subscriber. Ordinary UO pets are unaffected.

Designations are read at startup from `data/Sanctuary/Balance/facet-difficulties.json`, as an array of records with numeric `map` IDs and `difficulty` values `Normal`, `Nightmare` or `Hell`. Internal/unknown maps, duplicate IDs and invalid difficulties are rejected. **The shipped array is empty.** The disposable test's map choices are not production assignments.

Blade Sentinel now has an automatic retained-stat adapter, preserving fractional injury and immutable missile data before and after weapon inheritance. Its spatial travel and offline-expiry choices remain open; see [the scoped checkpoint](../tools/reference/blade-lifecycle-native-evidence.json). Other representations still need adapters: timed barriers, revived monsters, Hydra/Decoy, and future Valkyrie/Druid/Warlock summons. A class completion certificate and complete summon parity remain pending. [Native verification and scope](../tools/reference/summon-facet-difficulty-native-evidence.json).

# Corpse Explosion ground targeting

Reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**, approved Sanctuary deviations first. The user confirmed that ground clicks detonate a nearby corpse and approved a two-UO-tile tolerance. The [decision](../tools/reference/corpse-explosion-user-targeting-decision.json) preserves both statements. The exact native click boundary is unmeasured; cursor-nearest ranking within an inclusive Euclidean circle and lowest-serial exact ties are explicit Sanctuary policy.

The shared near-click picker now supports both mobiles and corpses through the same candidate ranking. Bone Prison retains its deterministic ties; Psychic Hammer retains its existing unverified-tie refusal. Corpse Explosion filters through its existing supported base-life/seed and corpse-access rules before ranking. Owned, nonselectable, shattered, spent, inaccessible, wrong-facet/height and missing-history corpses cannot obstruct a farther eligible candidate. Selection does not reserve a corpse, alter loot, roll or advance its source stream, or spend mana.

An empty-ground Corpse Explosion begins with the selected corpse's identity and location. Repeated input during windup cannot replace that pending corpse or timing. Release rechecks the existing corpse gates, then reuses the established fractional do55 physical/fire packet, corpse consumption, loot preservation, one source-stream advance and one payment. A corpse reserved during windup fails release without silently reacquiring another corpse. No eligible corpse means no cast or payment. The existing eight-tile caster-to-corpse range remains; this change is click tolerance, not a new range or damage formula.

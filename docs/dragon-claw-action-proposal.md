# Dragon Claw action proposal

Status: **approved 2026-11-08 JST**; see [the recorded approval](dragon-claw-approved-action-rule.md). The table retains its original proposal wording for traceability. Mechanical reference: **D2R Reign of the Warlock 3.3.93847**. Approval of Dragon Talon does not automatically extend to this skill. Exact modern per-contact charge handling and primary-hand chance-to-hit sharing remain unverified; the rules below would be explicit Sanctuary adaptations.

The prepared supplier now checks 1,152 eligible original-author dual-claw rate controls and 192 complete older sequence-clock controls in the native server. Real IAS equipment, different primary/secondary weapon speeds, selected-hand restoration, effective Dragon Claw rank and hard-only Claw Mastery damage synergy are covered. Its current calculator profile is distinct from Fists of Fire and Dragon Talon. See [preparation checkpoint](dragon-claw-preparation-checkpoint.md).

| Boundary | Proposed rule | Reference or uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligibility | Require two active eligible claw weapons on an ordinary Assassin. | Current `itypea1/itypeb1=h2h`, `weapsel=3`; shared native dual-claw eligibility. |
| Timing | Capture the prepared dual-claw clock at entry. At base advance 256, primary contact is frame 6, secondary contact frame 10, completion frame 16. | Existing complete sequence scheduler; pinned original calculator combines mean WSM and separately diminished hand IAS before one truncation. Exact modern scheduling/stat-mutation behavior remains unverified. |
| Payment | Spend two mana once at accepted entry. Underfunded use is one ordinary attack. Cancelling does not refund accepted payment. | Current two-mana table and existing shared action/payment/fallback services. |
| Target | Keep the initial enemy. Stop remaining contacts if it dies, disappears, becomes ineligible or leaves reach. No replacement target within a sequence. | Explicit target-loss adaptation, using existing melee target checks. |
| Hit result | Roll each contact separately in its selected hand. Active martial charges bypass accuracy only; shield block/dodge remain. The second contact uses whatever charges remain after the first. | The old packet builder executes per contact. Modern primary-roll sharing and charged defense boundaries remain uncertain; this is a defined adaptation. |
| Damage | Each hand supplies its own weapon/typed/poison/item packet. Skill ED is current `calc1`: effective Dragon Claw rank plus four percent per hard Claw Mastery point. Reuse Tiger/Cobra and existing progressive elemental helpers. | Current pinned formula and older packet/hand composition; complete native delivery still needs verification. Ordinary weapon critical rules remain applicable; kick-only hit flags are not copied. |
| Charges | Each successful, unblocked contact releases and consumes one charge from each active family. Misses/blocks preserve them; fully absorbed successful contact counts. | Explicit consume-one adaptation, using the existing ledger. The older oracle's full-list removal is not ported. |
| Lifecycle | Movement, Stop, changed equipped pair, hit recovery, invalid owner state, death, respec and disconnect stop future contacts. Reserve ordinals before callbacks; no replay/refund. Transient series do not resume after restart. | Existing sequence/action services; actual integration must be checked. |

Approval would authorize the action policy above. Before exposing the talent, verify actual selected-hand packets, once-only fractional payment, contact/charge/block/miss/immunity and cancellation cases, real bindings/input and two actual reloads. Full original geometry/RNG/event/item/proc/owner/PvP/client acceptance remains separate. Final dual-claw sequence/contact/charge-release effects and audio, plus talent and left/right/quick icons, remain identified placeholders in their separate registers.

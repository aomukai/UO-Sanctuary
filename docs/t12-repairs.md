# T12 — player and NPC repair/recharge

All six defaults were confirmed on 20 October 2026 (JST). Implemented, tested and deployed to the local runtime. The live server is running with the completed profession/crafting features enabled.

- A repair restores current durability to the remaining maximum after its possible loss. Maximum durability has a floor of1; broken gear remains repairable. Natural non-wearing items and cosmetic cloth clothing acquire no artificial durability.
- Exact processed subtype costs remain Normal1–3, Exceptional3–5, Elite5–10. Metal/leather/cloth additionally cost1/3/5 generic scraps/strips; wood requires matching boards without chips, bone requires bones without scraps. White needs no essence; Magic rolls the same tier bands and Rare doubles its essence roll. Unique uses a reserved3× multiplier when Unique items gain an implementation; T12 does not introduce Unique generation or a new essence item.
- Each committed action rolls once to save the entire material/essence bill. Skill below80 /80–<100 /100–<120 /120 gives10/20/25/30%. Tool uses and NPC fees still apply. Full quoted resources must be available before rolling. No repair skill gains.
- Durability loss remains50% for1–3 below80;25% for1–2 at80–<100;10% for1–2 at100–<120;5% for1 at120. NPC services work at80, including20% resource saving. Charge use and charge-only restoration do not lower maximum durability.
- Player work needs the active matching craft and its public workshop: repair hammer/Blacksmithing (with forge and anvil), tailoring repair kit/Tailoring, fletching tools/wood, tinker's tools/jewellery. Recharge follows the item's repair profession. One use covers a completed single or combined action, retaining crafted tools'10% use saving. New repair tools use the existing vendor10gold/20–30 uses and crafted100/110/120 capacity model. Tinkering recipes provisionally require25 skill: hammer2 iron+1 Metal Scrap; tailoring repair kit2 iron+1 Leather Strip.
- Matching town merchants offer repair/recharge through their context menu. Players supply backpack resources and pay100/500/2500 gold by base tier once per completed single or combined action. No personal tool or matching active craft is needed for NPC services.
- Recharge supports working native charged wands, a selected quantity or fill, and1 Magic Essence per charge. New wands persist their initial rolled charge capacity; legacy wands without a record use their native constructor's type ceiling, preserving any larger existing charge count. Partial recharges preserve the maximum. Tools and consumable tomes cannot be refilled. Sanctuary charged affixes remain gated until their actual spell consumers/acquisition are implemented.

## Transactions and persistence

`repairs.json` holds tier costs, skill brackets, tool recipes, fees and recharge cost. `SanctuaryRepairs` version0 stores quoted durability-material/essence draws and wand capacities. Reopening, cancelling, changing repairer and save/reload preserve the quote for the same damaged item state. A new accepted repair or new damage/material/tier/rarity state requires its own quote. Charge quantity and NPC versus player service do not reroll durability costs.

Confirmation shows actual resources, fee, requested charges and the durability-loss/saving odds. Item identity/ownership/parent/state, damage, material, charges, tool uses/parent, profession/skill/workshop, connection and NPC proximity/access are checked again before consumption. A request can commit only once; rejected requests consume no resources, fees or tool uses and draw no saving/loss outcome. Material resource properties and concrete subtypes both match, preventing another coloured resource from satisfying the bill.

The player entry points are repair-tool menus and `[Repair`; native repair menus route into the same flow, and native repair deeds cannot bypass resource costs for class characters. Town services share the same transaction. Chitin/Powder of Fortifying and maximum-durability restoration remain T36.

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a production sprite atlas for an isometric ARPG freezing breath channel.
Create ONE square transparent RGBA image, strictly uniform 4×4 grid of 16 separated cutout sprites. Four rows are four effects, four columns are successive animation frames. No letters, no dividing lines, no people, no background, no ground plane. Render only each effect on actual alpha, keep the important ice strokes opaque, clear 10% gutters, consistent pivots. Hand-painted crisp pixel clusters, muted pale blue and slate, white ice crystals and dark turquoise shadows. Isometric 3/4 perspective. Design for classic 50–120px gameplay sprites that must stand out on warm stone and grass with clear silhouette.
Row 1: thin compact mouth-of-wind release at a caster, pointing to the RIGHT in all four phases. Begin with two tiny curled bands of ice, expand into short parted cone, then a clean narrow backward tail with gust bands heading right. This is the icy origin of a stream, slender with a stable left origin, not a ring.
Row 2: traveling continuous-looking freezing stream segments, horizontal aimed to the RIGHT in all four phases. Three braided pale blue filament ribbons, narrow left trails and a clear pointed icy mist head toward the right. Four equally long true repeating phases with differing flakes and ribbon shapes, roughly 3:1 wide-to-tall aspect and stable pivot along centerline. Sparse dark edges plus bright core points, transparent gaps.
Row 3: terminal vapor at very small gameplay scale, a little icy puff with a few bright ice crystals that spreads into distinct puffs and dissolves to stray snow flecks. Stable central ground pivot; no floor or circle.
Row 4: frost contact spark, four successive cels of sharp small chips that flick upward and outward from a single hit and trail off; starts with a fine icy cross, opens into separated pale shard points, becomes scattered fine crystals at last. Keep the distinct final ice pixels visible.

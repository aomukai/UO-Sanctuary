Asset type: production isometric animated hurricane spell atlas for an existing dark fantasy pixel game.

Palette icy gray-blue, ice white highlights, dark blue-green cores, sparse frost particles that register at 50-200px gameplay size. Visual theme ghosts of cold wind carrying sharp snowy flakes.

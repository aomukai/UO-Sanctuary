Use case: stylized-concept. Sprite sheet for an original isometric vintage pixel-art RPG. Exactly four columns and four rows of sixteen completely separate sprites in equal cells on the square canvas. Genuine alpha-zero transparency between and around all elements and inside gaps, no opaque effects canvases. Locked center anchors throughout each row. Strong readable silhouettes and isolated crisp dithered edges, readable when converted to single-bit transparency and reduced to 25 pixels. Each row is a distinct effect with four frames in left-to-right animation order. No gridlines, text, numbers, labels, hardware objects, weapon, people, ground tile, opaque smoke, modern gradients, or UI. Siphon weapon hex, rich dark teal and petrol blue with a few cold mint highlights. Row one, two tiny damp teal trails curling inward then turning outward in a casting flash. Row two, an open sideways double leaf shape with mint flecks orbiting an empty point at a held weapon. Row three, a small hollow drop-shaped seal with two opposite sweeping ticks around it, teal with a mint highlight. Row four, a pair of thin turquoise recovery trails rising and curling around an empty center, originating low, rising, scattering, then dissipating to three or four small isolated sparks. Avoid filled magical circles and ordinary ocean imagery.

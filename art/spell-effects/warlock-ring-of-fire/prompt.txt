Use case: stylized-concept. Production game VFX sprite sheet for Sanctuary, a 2D 45-degree isometric pixel-art action RPG, viewed over a busy ground tile. The entire canvas is genuinely transparent with alpha 0 between and around sprites; clean isolated ash and ember silhouettes, no scene, no background wash, no black backing. Square canvas, exact four evenly spaced columns and three rows, twelve equal cells; centered silhouettes, strong empty gutters so each sprite can be sliced individually. One short restrained four-frame animation per row, moving left to right; consistent identity, silhouettes, ground line and camera. Warm coal orange, oxidized red, desaturated gold cores; selective high-value pixels, vintage painterly pixel art that reads when scaled down. No labels, text, objects, people, ground tile, captions, outlines of the grid or cast shadows. Ring of Fire. Row 1 is a small twisting ember halo at the caster's feet, forming from crumbs, flaring, dispersing. Row 2 is ONE low, compact cluster of three tongues of red and amber fire viewed front-facing on the isometric floor, left/right silhouette balanced, licking sideways across the floor through four animation poses. We repeat this sprite at multiple true missile centers to form a spreading ring, so keep it compact. Row 3 is a short vertical scarlet contact flash with a bright ember core, appearing then breaking into four soot-orange sparks. This is an ember spell, softer and lower than a wall of fire.

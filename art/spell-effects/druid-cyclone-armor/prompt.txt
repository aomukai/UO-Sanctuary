Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a production sprite atlas for an isometric dark fantasy cyclone shield.
Create one square TRUE transparent RGBA image with exactly 16 separate sprite cutouts in a uniform 4×4 grid. One effect in each row, four frames in consecutive columns, identical cell sizes and ground pivots. At least 12 percent transparent safety margin, no letters, mouse UI, shield icon, bars, people, solid backdrops, or floor. Crisp vintage isometric hand-painted pixel clusters. Desaturated blue-gray, slate teal, soft sage-green edges and antique silver with a few warm dust motes, opaque enough to read on a grass or sandstone tile, clearly distinct from a bright icy or electric shield. View from 3/4 top down. A shield made from moving ribbons of *air* with large negative spaces. Always leave the entire center transparent so a player's body stays visible. Clear background between all ribbons.
Row 1, Raise: first a few ground-level curls, then hair-thin ribbons rise along both sides of the center, then a wind sheath closes around its empty center, then nearly settled with a few small rushes. Think of two distinct open crescents framing the left and right edges, not a solid orb.
Row 2, Active rotating shield: four equal-width loop phases of two narrow silver-sage ribbons circling the player's sides at knee and shoulder level, in a wide oval slightly taller than wide. Shape, base and top dimensions stay locked across all four frames. Visibly move small leaves and curl tips. Interior must be at least 60 percent completely transparent, with separated bright traced arcs on both sides. Skinny wisps, not dense overlapping loops.
Row 3, Absorb: same scale and shape profile. A sudden flash and short wind wave across the rim of the shield, then a brighter spray of a few pale teal and white motes, then separated little glints shrinking through two frames; green-blue air highlights, no blast flame or lightning. Keep center open.
Row 4, Depletion: the shield breaks as if unravelling. Two stronger coils split out from their frame, four distinct curled strips peel away from the center, fragmented torn ribbons separate further, then a few last pale blue curls and dust motes disperse into the open center. No text and all four cels remain legible at 90px wide.

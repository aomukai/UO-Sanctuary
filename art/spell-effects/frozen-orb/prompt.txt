Original production sprite sheet for Frozen Orb, a dark-fantasy isometric action RPG magic projectile. Four rows by four columns, equal square cells, generous transparent gutters, 16 frame sprites on true transparent alpha. No grids, text, panel borders, shadows unrelated to the sprite, backgrounds or characters. Hand-painted crisp clustered pixel art, designed to remain legible when downscaled to 20–55 pixels. Restrained moon-white, cold cyan, and deep prussian blue. Read rows left to right. Row 1: four sequential frames of a jagged spherical core of bright ice with 3 orbiting frozen wisps, rotating smoothly around the same center at the same apparent size. Strong deep blue shadow in the core and pale asymmetric facets. Row 2: four sequential frames of a narrow luminous ice splinter flying exactly horizontally to the right, pale point on right, faint tail to left. Same center and same proportions through each frame. Row 3: four sequential frames of a spherical ice core cracking into a radial burst of light blue ice fragments, expanding from a very small initial footprint, then contracting into sparks by frame 4. Row 4: four sequential frames of a very small blow of snowy powder around a white center, fading sharply as individual ice grains disappear. Distinct polished silhouettes and short restrained particle trails, no glow spilling past cell edges. Original treatment designed for this game.

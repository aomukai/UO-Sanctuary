Use case: stylized-concept. Sprite sheet for an original isometric vintage pixel-art RPG. Exactly four columns and four rows of sixteen completely separate sprites in equal cells on the square canvas. Genuine alpha-zero transparency between and around all elements and inside gaps, no opaque effects canvases. Locked center anchors throughout each row. Strong readable silhouettes and isolated crisp dithered edges, readable when converted to single-bit transparency and reduced to 25 pixels. Each row is a distinct effect with four frames in left-to-right animation order. No gridlines, text, numbers, labels, hardware objects, weapon, people, ground tile, opaque smoke, modern gradients, or UI. Bane weapon hex, dark plum, amethyst, and silver-lilac small highlights. Row one, four planes of a curved purple two-prong magical ribbon gathering around a point on release and dissipating. Row two, a compact pair of slim thorn curls orbiting a hollow point where a character's held weapon is, small and restrained. Row three, a tiny vertical thistle-shaped rune to attach to an enemy torso, ominous plum center and one luminous violet outline. Row four, three small skew diagonal purple glints making a fast compact contact slash, brighter at frame two then thins to stray sparks. Use distinct silhouettes and strongly clear background.

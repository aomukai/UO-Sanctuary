A transparent 1536×1024 raster sprite atlas for Sanctuary, a hand-painted isometric dark medieval fantasy action RPG. Grid exactly four columns by two rows, eight individual isolated texture cells, each a complete sprite, large transparent gutters, no grid lines, text, symbols, environment or props. Both rows contain horizontal isometric concentric ground rings, approximately 1.9 times wider than tall, high readability when reduced, crisply defined slim bright contours. Row one is the Grim Ward's short outward fear-reach pulse in muted violet with a few small ember-gold sparks on the rim. Four equally spaced frames show a closed thin ring of hooked thorn wisps brightening, flaring, fragmenting, and dissipating. Transparent completely empty center for each frame. Keep the ellipse aligned and all ring shapes the same width and height across the four frames, the game engine scales the radius. Row two is a second subtler version using only lilac with almost no embers, as an alternate. Corner lights must always remain in the cell; avoid surrounding fog, dark vignettes, backdrop color, skulls or people.

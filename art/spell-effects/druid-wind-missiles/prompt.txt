Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production sprite atlas for an isometric dark fantasy game's grounded wind magic.
Create one square true transparent RGBA image containing exactly 16 untouching sprites in a uniform 4 by 4 grid. Each row is one effect and each column a successive animation frame. Equal cell sizes with consistent pivots. No lines, letters, shadows outside the art, floor, backdrop, characters, card frames or tint plates. All 16 isolated on transparent alpha, with at least 12% clear margins all around each cell. Single central object per cell. Isometric 3/4 down angle, hand-painted crisp pixel-cluster aesthetic, textured antique silver and slate-blue outlines with warm gray bounce, a few off-white highlights and wisps, clear at 48-130px gameplay size. Avoid transparent haze over the whole cell and avoid bright white masses. Genuine wind shapes made visible by dust and a few leaves.
Row 1 is small low Twister. Four equal-width loop phases of a narrow low cotton-silver eddy, twice as wide as tall, three curved ribbons with clearly separated gaps, a few burnt umber flecks; fixed feet at center of bottom ellipse and overall shape and silhouette stable apart from rotating ribbon and orbiting specks.
Row 2 is powerful Tornado. Four phase-matched loop cels of a larger tall steel-blue and cool silver funnel, narrow at the ground, much wider at its whirling top lip, scattered ground grit toward the base and layered interior. Keep silhouette size and alignment consistent, and visibly shift the ribbons and small debris with each consecutive frame. It must be a column above ground and visually distinct from row 1.
Row 3 is a compact grounded cast release for both wind missiles. First one curled silver ribbon just off the ground, then three broad concentric fragments of a thin broken spiral, then they fly outward as a horizontal flat elliptical ground-level sweep, and finally tiny separated remnants. Ellipse center does not move.
Row 4 is a short received hit effect, little horizontal silver split slash with a handful of beige grit motes, expands left and right like air splitting briefly, then dissolves to a few flecks. Make the final frame still visible at downsampled size. Keep it small so it will not conceal a character. Do not include any typography.

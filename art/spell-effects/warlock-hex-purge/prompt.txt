Use case: stylized-concept. Sprite sheet for an original isometric vintage pixel-art RPG. Exactly four columns and four rows of sixteen completely separate sprites in equal cells on the square canvas. Genuine alpha-zero transparency between and around all elements and inside gaps, no opaque effects canvases. Locked center anchors throughout each row. Strong readable silhouettes and isolated crisp dithered edges, readable when converted to single-bit transparency and reduced to 25 pixels. Each row is a distinct effect with four frames in left-to-right animation order. No gridlines, text, numbers, labels, hardware objects, weapon, people, ground tile, opaque smoke, modern gradients, or UI. Purge weapon hex. Rust-red copper, pale warm magnesium highlights and dark red-violet shadow. Row one, a squat copper-white angular casting flash opening in a small ring. Row two, two open bronze angular crescents revolving closely around an empty point for weapon enchantment. Row three, a compact cracked copper triangle with a small pinprick cream-white center for the affected enemy. Row four, a compact but forceful explosion of copper shards and warm white center, from tight sparks into an open radial spray with shafts going up and falling back to the floor, center low stable. Sparse enough to stay clear amid other combat effects.

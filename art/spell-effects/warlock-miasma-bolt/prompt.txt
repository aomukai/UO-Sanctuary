Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprite sheet for an original vintage isometric pixel-art RPG. Generate a square 4 by 4 grid of sixteen separate game effect sprites on genuinely transparent alpha-zero background. The canvas has exact equal cells, clear gutters, and four frames from left to right per row. Place each sprite around the center of its own cell with a stable anchor, without overlapping another cell. Brief pulses and strong shape readability at 20 to 60 pixels over busy stone floors. Subdued deep violet, twilight blue, oxidized indigo and occasional periwinkle core highlights. Dithered silhouette and point highlights, no decorative text, UI, background, ground tile, faces, runic letters, gore, or solid backing. Keep fog as isolated ragged thin strands with large transparent holes and no rectangular wash. Miasma Bolt. Row 1 four frames of a round gathering knot of purple wisps and floating blue flecks around a small caster-sized point, tightening then breaking. Row 2 four frames of one tiny pointed arcane projectile moving horizontally RIGHT, head at the right end of the sprite and faint tapering tail to the left, ghostly dark-purple core surrounded by two twisting periwinkle ribbons. Keep the same center and steady overall length so it can be rotated into eight game directions. Row 3 four phases of an asymmetrical small indigo burst impact, first tight and white-edged, then petals of drifting crescent splinters then dissipating. Row 4 four frames of one low near-ground open miasma cloud, looping thin lavender and indigo wisps with strongly transparent interior, slightly brighter on its edges than its body, isometric side view. Its art must remain visible in an old bitmap game without semitransparent fog.

Original production sprite sheet for Abyss, an isometric dark-fantasy action RPG spell that pulls enemies into a rift on the ground and collapses inward. Four columns by four rows of equally sized square cells with generous transparent gutters. Exactly 16 isolated frames on genuine transparent alpha. Fine late-1990s hand-painted pixel art, defined clustered edges legible when reduced to 60–110 pixels. Palette near-black, muted spectral violet and dim gray, small highlights of pale lavender. Each row left to right is a sequence on the same center and the ground plane, viewed in 2:1 isometric perspective. Row 1: a narrow hairline tear on the ground opens into an oval black hole rimmed by small violet split cracks. Row 2: four loop frames of the same deep oval pit with a thin uneven luminous violet rim and tiny bright flecks orbiting and drifting inward. Row 3: four loop frames of inward radial suction trails hugging the ground, a few wispy lavender streaks starting on the outer edges and converging on the transparent center, visually sparse. Row 4: four stages of collapse, rim buckles and shrinks inwards leaving a tight violet pop, then a dim halo, then a tiny nearly vanished fleck. Keep transparent outer margins, no walls, scenery, character, labels or frame lines. Original art with restrained brightness, no neon.

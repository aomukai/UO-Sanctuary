# Druid shapeshift sprites

The source sheets were generated using built-in ImageGen. Run `python3 tools/build_druid_form_animations.py` to pack the five directions and their movement, attack, cast, hit and fall poses for the client.

## Werebear

Reference concept `werebear.jpg`.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production isometric pixel sprite pose atlas for a dark-fantasy RPG. Design an original druid werebear taking broad creature cues from the attached mood reference. Biped, very wide shoulders, short blunt grizzly muzzle, small round ears, heavy dark russet fur, huge blunt-pawed arms, a short ragged umber waist wrap. Natural eyes and weathered ash-brown shadows, compact silhouette and feet visible even from behind. Render as richly textured hand-pixelled late-1990s prerendered game sprites, with warm upper-left light and neutral background-free material shading. The figure must stay recognizable at 70-85 screen pixels high. Columns exactly five game views left-to-right: directly toward camera from lower edge (south), toward lower-left (southwest), left profile (west), facing away toward upper-left (northwest), direct back (north). Tilt the orthographic camera about 35 degrees downward. Repeat these exact views in all rows. Rows exactly five: neutral fighting idle with both feet down, left foot forward and right back walking, right foot forward and left back walking, a broad forward claw strike, collapsed on the ground facing the same orientation. Each of the 25 figures has a distinct generous transparent gutter and fully visible paws and head. Disciplined regular centered 5 by 5 grid, square overall canvas, equal cells. No border, separators, shadow on floor, staging, props, effects, lettering or palette samples. True alpha transparency.

# Lazarus’s mausoleum

Use `[go Lazarus` to arrive at **6976, 3746 (0)** in Trammel. Lazarus and his two bodyguards stand at the altar ahead. The fire circle lies 39 tiles west and five tiles south of the altar. The shortest distance between consecutive circles ranges from 34 to 46 tiles. Going around a pier adds to the run. [Entrance](arrival.jpg) · [Altar and steps](chancel-view.jpg) · [Fire circle](fire-circle.jpg) · [Fourth column from the cold circle](cold-column.jpg)

The chamber painting uses the repository sketch for the placement of the altar and five circles. Four piers flank the central crossing. The ImageGen brief called for a Romanesque undercroft with round arches, skull niches, smoke-dark stone and a clear central fighting floor. ClassicUO draws the registered texture at twice its stored resolution, with Lazarus’s tile centered at source pixel (980, 1880). Three masked sprites give the four piers depth against passing characters. The near and far piers share a sprite and have independent draw positions. Braziers, the sacrificial altar and the pentagrams are separate world items. The server supplies the walkable floor, perimeter and solid pier footprints. ## Paving and altar

Each square patch of paving spans seven UO tiles along each map axis and contains fourteen courses. The renderer’s 22-pixel ground vectors determine its projection. The bake preserves the lighting around the piers and burial niches. To rebuild the embedded chamber texture, run `node tools/bake_mausoleum_paving.mjs` with FFmpeg on the path. [Paving material](sources/paving.jpg) · [Projection tool](../../../tools/bake_mausoleum_paving.mjs)

The long edges of the chancel run east–west along Britannia’s X axis. The short sides follow Y. The three shallow limestone steps retain their original dimensions and clipped corners. Each step adds two units of world elevation. The upper landing supports the altar, Lazarus, and the two guards. The stair artwork uses the same outlines as the walkable surfaces. On upgrade, floor occupants follow their tread up or down. Anyone logged out on the new altar footprint returns in the aisle beside it. [Stair geometry](chancel.json) · [Painted stonework](sources/chancel.png)

The 128 × 160 pixel altar stands in the middle of the landing and runs parallel to the east–west wall and the long stair edges. Red Vex and Blackjade stand off its opposite ends. Five low collision stones follow the base along one row. The registration script projects the painted slab and carved faces onto the two UO tile axes.
